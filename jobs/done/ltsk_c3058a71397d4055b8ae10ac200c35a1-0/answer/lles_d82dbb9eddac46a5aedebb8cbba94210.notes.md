# TOPIC: EXPRESSION OF THE FUTURE
## SUB-TOPIC: Using unless

### New words
- unless, condition, if not, comma.

### Using unless
- Unless means “if not”.
- A comma follows an unless-clause placed at the beginning.
- A comma is not required before unless in the middle.
<!-- source: lchk_66e2ee5447794359a38eab8dca9d1b53 "“Unless” means “if not”" -->

### Example 1
- If you don’t go to bed early, you will feel tired.
- Unless you go to bed early, you will get tired.
<!-- source: lchk_66e2ee5447794359a38eab8dca9d1b53 "Unless you go to bed early, you will get tired" -->

### Example 2
- Unless he listens to his teacher, he won’t learn.
- The condition comes first.
- A comma separates the condition from the main clause.
<!-- source: lchk_52d32128a6914ca8ae03917f25c7b78f "Unless he listens to his teacher, he won’t learn" -->

### Example 3
- Do not open this booklet unless you are told to do so.
- The unless-clause comes after the main clause.
- No comma is needed before unless here.
<!-- source: lchk_11d1b263ed194f0dbe2283cd505309d9 "DO NOT OPEN THIS BOOKLET UNLESS YOU ARE TOLD TO DO SO" -->
