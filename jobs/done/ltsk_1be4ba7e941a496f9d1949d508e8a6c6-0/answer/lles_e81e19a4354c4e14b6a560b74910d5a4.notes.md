# TOPIC: LENGTH, MASS AND CAPACITY
## SUB-TOPIC: Area of shaded rectangles

### New words
- outer rectangle, inner rectangle, shaded area, square units.

### Area of a shaded rectangle
- $\text{Rectangle area}=\text{length}\times\text{width}$.
- $\text{Shaded area}=\text{outer area}-\text{inner area}$.
- Area is measured in square units.
<!-- source: lchk_fe78f42f00684caa943bea35cdbfc4d2 "Area of shaded part" -->

### Example: outer and inner areas
- The outer rectangle measures $10\text{ cm}$ by $9\text{ cm}$.
- The inner rectangle measures $6\text{ cm}$ by $4\text{ cm}$.
- $\text{Outer area}=10\times9=90\text{ cm}^2$.
- $\text{Inner area}=6\times4=24\text{ cm}^2$.
- $\text{Shaded area}=90-24=66\text{ cm}^2$.
<!-- source: lchk_35e370d976c340a69e206bdcaf99d939 "Area of shaded part = 90cm 2" -->

### Example: a larger rectangular frame
- The outer rectangle measures $60\text{ cm}$ by $50\text{ cm}$.
- The inner rectangle measures $40\text{ cm}$ by $35\text{ cm}$.
- $\text{Outer area}=60\times50=3000\text{ cm}^2$.
- $\text{Inner area}=40\times35=1400\text{ cm}^2$.
- $\text{Shaded area}=3000-1400=1600\text{ cm}^2$.
