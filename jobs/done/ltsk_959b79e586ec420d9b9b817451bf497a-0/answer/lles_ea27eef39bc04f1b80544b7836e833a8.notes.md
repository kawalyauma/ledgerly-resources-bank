# TOPIC: UGANDA AS AN INDEPENDENT NATION
## SUB-TOPIC: INDEPENDENCE

### Meaning of independence
- Independence is freedom from foreign control or rule.
- Political independence allows citizens to govern their own country.
- A nation is a community of people living under one government.
- A state is an organised political community ruled by a government.
<!-- source: lchk_1162553670784949a6a5f4ead61c3374 "state of being free from foreign control or rule" -->

### Characteristics of an independent nation
- An independent nation has its own government.
- It has a recognised territory of land.
- It has citizens who belong to the country.
- It has a constitution to guide the nation.
- It has a parliament to make laws.
- It has a judiciary to interpret laws.
- The police maintain law and order.
- Its citizens enjoy freedom from foreign rule.
- It controls its own affairs and decisions.
<!-- source: lchk_1162553670784949a6a5f4ead61c3374 "Characteristics of an independent nation" -->

### Uganda's independence
- Uganda gained independence on 9 October 1962.
- The London constitutional conference prepared the way for independence.
- Uganda People's Congress led Uganda to independence.
- Dr Apollo Milton Obote was independent Uganda's first prime minister.
- The 1962 Constitution granted Uganda independence.
- The Union Jack was lowered on Independence Day.
<!-- source: lchk_b6ebfff8d01745efa6141743d019e6df "Which constitution granted Uganda independence" -->

### A republic and its leadership
- A republic is a country led by a president under its laws.
- Uganda became a republic in 1967.
- A president is the country's head of state.
- Sir Edward Muteesa II became Uganda's first president in 1963.

### Note
- Independence Day in Uganda is 9 October.
- Citizens control their affairs in an independent country.
