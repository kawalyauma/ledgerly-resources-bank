# TOPIC: UGANDA AS AN INDEPENDENT NATION
## SUB-TOPIC: THE NATIONAL COAT OF ARMS

### The Uganda national coat of arms
- The coat of arms is a national symbol of Uganda.
- It identifies Uganda on official government documents.
- It is a symbol of national power and authority.
- Paul Mukasa designed the Uganda national coat of arms.
<!-- source: lchk_0f400a3753174ca4964cfae5fee9eca7 "The Uganda coat of arms was designed by Paul Mukasa." -->

### Features of the coat of arms
[[diagram:uganda-coat-of-arms-p5-week1]]
- The coat of arms contains the crested crane.
- It contains the Uganda kob.
- The central shield includes the sun.
- The shield is crossed by two spears.
- Coffee and cotton appear among its features.
- A drum lies below the shield.
- Blue water stripes appear on the shield.
- It stands above a green landscape.
- The national motto appears beneath the features.
<!-- source: lchk_6816a0521efb434a8d222966ed00fd03 "Features on the coat of arms" -->

### Meaning of the main features
- The crested crane is Uganda's national emblem.
- The kob represents Uganda's rich wildlife.
- The sun shows Uganda's abundant sunshine.
- Coffee and cotton represent agricultural cash crops.
- The shield and spears represent traditional defence.
- The drum represents traditional culture and communication.
- The blue stripes represent lakes and River Nile waters.
<!-- source: lchk_6816a0521efb434a8d222966ed00fd03 "They represent the traditional weapons used by Ugandans for defence." -->

### Where the coat of arms appears
- It appears on Ugandan paper currency and coins.
- It appears on official government letters.
- It appears on the government seal.
- It can be displayed in courts and offices.
- It may be displayed on the president's official car.
<!-- source: lchk_6816a0521efb434a8d222966ed00fd03 "Uses of coat of arms" -->

### Note
- The shield and spears show traditional defence.
- The coat of arms represents Uganda's identity and authority.
