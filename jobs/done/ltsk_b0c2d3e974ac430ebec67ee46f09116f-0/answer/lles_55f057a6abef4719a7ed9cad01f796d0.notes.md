# TOPIC: POPULATION, SIZE AND DISTRIBUTION
## SUB-TOPIC: HIGH AND LOW POPULATION DENSITY

### Remember
- LOW POPULATION DENSITY. This is when an area has a small number of people compared to the available resources .
- Of what importance is a high population to the following
- HIGH POPULATION DENSITY. This is a large number of people living in an area compared to the available resources . Page 47 Areas with a High population density.
- Communicable diseases can not easily spread from one homestead to another. Disadvantages of a low population density.
- Areas that have a good climate attract a high population density
- Areas that have fertile soils attract high population density to practice agriculture.
- High population creates large market for goods.
- High population leads to high tax base.
