# TOPIC: LENGTH, MASS AND CAPACITY
## SUB-TOPIC: Converting metric length units

### New words
- metre, decimetre, centimetre, millimetre.

### Metres and centimetres
- $1\text{ m}=100\text{ cm}$.
- $1\text{ m}=1000\text{ mm}$.
- $100\text{ cm}=1\text{ m}$.
<!-- source: lchk_03f8b377dc464cf8a3543c4f833ea50d "Metres to centimeters" -->

### Example: metres to centimetres
- Convert $4\text{ m}$ to centimetres.
- $1\text{ m}=100\text{ cm}$.
- $4\text{ m}=4\times100\text{ cm}=400\text{ cm}$.
- Express $86\text{ m}$ as centimetres.
- $86\text{ m}=86\times100\text{ cm}$.
- $86\text{ m}=8600\text{ cm}$.
<!-- source: lchk_03f8b377dc464cf8a3543c4f833ea50d "Express 86m as cm" -->

### Example: centimetres to metres
- Write $6200\text{ cm}$ as metres.
- $100\text{ cm}=1\text{ m}$.
- $6200\text{ cm}=6200\div100\text{ m}=62\text{ m}$.
- Change $530\text{ cm}$ to metres.
- $530\text{ cm}=530\div100\text{ m}$.
- $530\text{ cm}=5.3\text{ m}$.
<!-- source: lchk_03f8b377dc464cf8a3543c4f833ea50d "Change 530cm to m" -->

### Example: metres to millimetres
- Change $7\text{ m}$ to millimetres.
- $1\text{ m}=1000\text{ mm}$.
- $7\text{ m}=7\times1000\text{ mm}$.
- $7\text{ m}=7000\text{ mm}$.
