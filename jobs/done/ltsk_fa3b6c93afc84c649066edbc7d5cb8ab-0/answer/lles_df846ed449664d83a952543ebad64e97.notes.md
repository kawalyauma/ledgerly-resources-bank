# TOPIC: LENGTH, MASS AND CAPACITY
## SUB-TOPIC: Unknown sides, area and perimeter

### New words
- opposite sides, unknown, equation, substitution.

### Comparing opposite sides
- The opposite sides of a rectangle are equal.
- $\text{Area}=\text{length}\times\text{width}$.
- $\text{Perimeter}=2(\text{length}+\text{width})$.
<!-- source: lchk_2141da57e5bd4c278b8a405ae7cf3c0f "Rectangle : is a quadrilateral with two opposite sides equal" -->

### Example: finding the unknown
- ABCD is a rectangle: $AB=(2x-5)\text{ cm}$, $DC=(x+3)\text{ cm}$, $AD=(x-1)\text{ cm}$.
- $2x-5=x+3$.
- $2x-5+5=x+3+5$.
- $2x=x+8$.
- $2x-x=x-x+8$.
- $x=8$.
<!-- source: lchk_c4287e2236664f34876c77b8819206e1 "ABCD is a rectangle" -->

### Example: actual length and width
- Length is $(x+3)\text{ cm}$.
- $L=8+3=11\text{ cm}$.
- Width is $(x-1)\text{ cm}$.
- $W=8-1=7\text{ cm}$.
<!-- source: lchk_fe78f42f00684caa943bea35cdbfc4d2 "What is the length and width of the figure" -->

### Example: area and perimeter
- $A=L\times W$.
- $A=11\text{ cm}\times7\text{ cm}=77\text{ cm}^2$.
- $P=2(L+W)$.
- $P=2(11\text{ cm}+7\text{ cm})$.
- $P=2\times18\text{ cm}=36\text{ cm}$.
