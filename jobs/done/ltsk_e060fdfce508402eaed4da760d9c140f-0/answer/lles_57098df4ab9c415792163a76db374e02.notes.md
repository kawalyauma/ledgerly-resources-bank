# TOPIC: CHRISTIAN INVOLVEMENT IN THE WORLD
## SUB-TOPIC: IMPORTANCE OF WORKING TOGETHER

### Meaning of cooperation
- Cooperation means working together to achieve a desired goal.
- People cooperate by working together as a team.
- Cooperation encourages concern for the welfare of others.
- Cooperation encourages people to care for God's creation.
<!-- source: lchk_48579250e06e49c3945066f2fdf59bf0 "Cooperation means working together to achieve a desired goal" -->

### Importance of working together
- Cooperation helps people overcome challenges.
- Working together builds strength among people.
- People receive new ideas from other people.
- Teamwork helps people succeed in shared projects.
- Cooperation helps create a better world.
- Cooperation encourages peace and freedom for all.
- Cooperation develops concern for the welfare of others.
<!-- source: lchk_2fb7f3526b2948ab9b25f475235d0794 "Why do we need to co-operate with each other?" -->

### Values that support cooperation
- Unselfishness means considering the welfare of other people.
- Being generous is an example of unselfishness.
- Being loving is an example of unselfishness.
- Being just promotes fairness to other people.
- Unselfishness produces peace and cooperation.
- Good relationships help Christians care for others.
<!-- source: lchk_dc3683396e644a59a83c58027137a967 "Examples of unselfishness" -->

### Christian examples of working for others
- Jesus visited and healed the sick.
- Jesus fed people who were hungry.
- Jesus forgave people who sinned.
- Jesus comforted people who were sorrowful.
- Jesus kept lonely people company.
- Christians can support people in difficult situations.
<!-- source: lchk_dc3683396e644a59a83c58027137a967 "Jesus as an example of unselfishness" -->
