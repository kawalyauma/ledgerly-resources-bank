# TOPIC: OUR LEADERS IN THE DISTRICT
## SUB-TOPIC: BY-LAWS — PART B

### Remember
- By-laws are local laws made by local councils.
- They help local councils settle community disputes.
- They promote peace, security and order.
- They promote respect for people's rights.
- Village councils protect people and their property.
- Village councils promote sanitation and unity.
- Ignorance may hinder enforcement of local laws.
- Shortage of funds is a problem for district leaders.
