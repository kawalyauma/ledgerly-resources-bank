# TOPIC: EXPRESSION OF THE FUTURE
## SUB-TOPIC: Using for

### New words
- for, duration, hours, years, present perfect.

### For expresses duration
- For shows the length of time an action has lasted.
- An hour is a duration.
- Four hours is a duration.
- Two years is a duration.
- Five hours is a duration.
<!-- source: lchk_52d32128a6914ca8ae03917f25c7b78f "For is used when the length of time" -->

### Example 1
- They have played for four hours.
- I have not seen my mother for two years.
- Joseph has sat there for five hours.
<!-- source: lchk_52d32128a6914ca8ae03917f25c7b78f "They have played for four hours" -->

### Example 2: comparing for and since
- The baby has cried for an hour.
- The boy has been crying since 9:00 a.m.
- For introduces the duration.
- Since introduces the starting time.
<!-- source: lchk_52d32128a6914ca8ae03917f25c7b78f "The baby has cried for an hour" -->
