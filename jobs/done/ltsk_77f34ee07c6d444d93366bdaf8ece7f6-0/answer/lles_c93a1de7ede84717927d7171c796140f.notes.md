# TOPIC: UGANDA AS AN INDEPENDENT NATION
## SUB-TOPIC: BAD PRACTICES DURING ELECTIONS

### Meaning and key facts about bad practices during elections
- Campaigning on the election day
- Grabbing or stealing the ballot boxes. Bad practice after elections
- Bribing voters during elections
- Violence during elections /fighting
- Torturing of opponents.
- Making fun of the losers of an election.
- Mis-recording of election results.
- A voter who registers more than once
- Giving wrong information about the voter

### Features and related facts about bad practices during elections
- Misdirecting the voters
- Recording wrong information about voters
- Making it impossible for voters to vote
- Unfair splitting of constitutions/ gerrymandering
- Manipulation of the population make up
- Blocking candidates from campaigning freely
- Giving false information about a partner candidate / smear campaign.
- Intimidating voters viii) Omission of voter names.
- Creation of ghost polling station.

### Further facts about bad practices during elections
- Ballot stuffing (putting pre-ticked ballot papers in the ballot box before elections)
- A candidate refusing to accept defeat.
- Attacking a voter who has not voted a particular candidate.
- Unnecessary delay to announce final results. Page 17
- Intimidation of voters
- Grabbing and stealing ballot papers.
- Campaigning on the voting day.
- Cheating when counting votes.
