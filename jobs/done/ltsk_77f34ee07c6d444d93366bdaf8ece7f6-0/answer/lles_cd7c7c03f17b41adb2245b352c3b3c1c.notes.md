### Further facts about elections
- It organizes a by – election
- This is a system of election where voters use ballot papers to choose leaders.
- General election. This is a formal democratic process in which people choose leaders at the national level eg. The president, members of parliament.
- Referendum election. This is a formal democratic process in which people choose to accept or reject a political proposal that concerns all citizens in the country.
- Local election. This is a formal democratic process in which people choose leaders at local council level ie. Local council I -V leaders.
- By-election. This is a formal democratic process of choosing a leader to fill a vacant political post.
- Cancellation of the election results by the court of law.
- Making fun of the losers of an election.
- Mis-recording of election results.
