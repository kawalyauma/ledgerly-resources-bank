# TOPIC: WITNESS — CHRIST THE LIGHT
## SUB-TOPIC: CHRIST AS THE LIGHT OF THE WORLD

### Jesus as the light of the world
<!-- source: lchk_af0983e9dd8d42c9818cd1d9093d8fb2 "What John means by Jesus as the light." -->
- **Christ the Light** is a Christian teaching about Jesus bringing life to the world.
- John 8:12 describes Jesus as the light of the world.
- Christians believe that people who follow Jesus receive the light of life.
- Jesus was sent by God the Father to bring life to people.
- The light of Christ is connected with forgiveness and salvation from sin.
- Jesus invited his followers to live by his teaching and example.

### Ways Jesus showed God's light
<!-- source: lchk_af0983e9dd8d42c9818cd1d9093d8fb2 "Ways Jesus was the light of the world." -->
- Jesus trained his disciples to become ready to serve others.
- He taught them to receive God's kingdom with the humility of children.
- He showed love for his friend Lazarus and grieved at his death.
- Jesus remained loyal and obedient to God during his mission.
- He healed the sick and brought comfort to people in difficulty.
- He fed hungry people who needed food and care.
- Jesus treated people with sympathy and compassion.
- He showed followers a pattern of loving and helping others.

### Bible teachings about light
<!-- source: lchk_93fa2d984d4d48f7a18355666d6c2601 "WAYS IN WHICH CHRIST IS THE LIGHT OF THE WORLD" -->
- John 8:12 teaches that Jesus is the light of the world.
- Matthew 5:14–16 urges followers to shine so others see their good deeds.
- Christians are expected to show kindness and goodness to others.
- The light of Jesus is reflected when believers forgive those who wrong them.
- Christians reflect his teachings by caring for the needy.
- Christian service includes sharing love and helping the sick.
- Respect for God and other people is part of reflecting Christ's light.
