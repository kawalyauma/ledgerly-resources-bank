# TOPIC: WITNESS — CHRIST THE LIGHT
## SUB-TOPIC: WEEK 1 REVIEW

### Summary: Christ the light
<!-- source: lchk_af0983e9dd8d42c9818cd1d9093d8fb2 "Christ is the light" -->
- Jesus describes himself as the light of the world in John 8:12.
- Jesus healed the sick, helped the hungry and served others.
- He taught disciples to be humble and loving.
- His care for Lazarus showed sympathy and love.
- Christians believe that Jesus brings life and guidance.

### Summary: Reflecting the light of Jesus
<!-- source: lchk_93fa2d984d4d48f7a18355666d6c2601 "Examples of how Christians can reflect the light of Jesus to others" -->
- Christians reflect Jesus' light through loving actions.
- They forgive people who wrong them.
- They help the poor, hungry and sick.
- Christian witness includes honesty, compassion and obedience.
- Kind service to others follows Jesus' teaching.
