# TOPIC: FRIENDS ON THE WAY
## SUB-TOPIC: MARRIAGE IN CHRISTIAN LIFE

### Meaning of marriage
<!-- source: lchk_232ef985adf24ac1a738940b5f92a701 "MARRIAGE" -->
- **Marriage** is a legal union of a man and a woman as husband and wife.
- The Christian lessons describe marriage as a commitment of mutual love.
- Marriage creates a family relationship with duties towards each partner.
- Holy Matrimony joins a husband and wife in the Christian community.
- Married partners are expected to treat one another with lasting respect.

### Types of marriage
<!-- source: lchk_232ef985adf24ac1a738940b5f92a701 "Types of marriage" -->
- **Customary marriage** follows the customs of the families concerned.
- Customary ceremonies may be presided over by clan and family leaders.
- **Civil marriage** is conducted before a government marriage official.
- The source identifies government registrars or CAOs for civil marriage.
- **Religious marriage** takes place before an authorised religious leader.
- Christian marriage services may be witnessed by a reverend or priest.

### Qualities of a good marriage partner
<!-- source: lchk_232ef985adf24ac1a738940b5f92a701 "Qualities of a good marriage partner." -->
- A faithful partner is honest and worthy of trust.
- A good partner shows respect, kindness and care.
- Patience and tolerance help partners handle everyday difficulties.
- Forgiveness can help married people recover from disagreements.
- Partners should be truthful, sincere and open with one another.
- Partners should work hard and help to support family needs.
- Love and faithfulness are qualities expected in Christian marriage.
- A caring partner treats the other person with dignity and understanding.

### Marriage vows
<!-- source: lchk_ce7cf5d8d98940d2a65961808b128398 "Marriage vows" -->
- **Marriage vows** are promises made by people entering marriage.
- The promises express their commitment to love each other.
- These promises may be made before a priest, pastor or reverend.
- Marriage vows include commitment in sickness and health.
- Marriage vows also mention difficult and comfortable circumstances.
- The source presents marriage as a relationship intended to last for life.
- Marriage requires faithfulness, trust, patience and care from both partners.
