# TOPIC: OUR LEADERS IN THE DISTRICT
## SUB-TOPIC: WAYS OF ACQUIRING LEADERSHIP — PART B

### Remember
- Political leaders can be selected through elections.
- Civic leaders can receive authority through appointment.
- Cultural leaders may inherit leadership.
- Voluntary leaders offer their services willingly.
- Some elections use a secret ballot system.
- Some elections use an open voting system.
- Ballot papers are used during elections.
- The ballot box keeps ballots after voting.
- The voters' register lists eligible voters.
- A polling station is the place where voting happens.
- Polling officers are in charge of polling stations.
- Election candidates stand to be voted into office.
