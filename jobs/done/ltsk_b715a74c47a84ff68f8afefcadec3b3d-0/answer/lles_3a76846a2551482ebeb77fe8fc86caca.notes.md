- Valley areas and plains have a low population due to floods and high temperatures. Social service provision.
- The areas with good vegetation cover like savannah grasslands are favourable for human settlement. Relief.
- Climate of the area.
- Fertility of the land.
- Areas with poor climatic conditions are sparsely populated.
- People migrate to urban areas where social services are available.
- Better health services lead to decreased death rates in an area.
- Population growth is the increase in the number of people living in an area. Causes of population growth
