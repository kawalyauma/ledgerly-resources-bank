## SUB-TOPIC: Traffic lights

### New words

### Three colours of the traffic lights
- Red
- Orange
- Green
<!-- source: lchk_e3d07b1570aa426dbf34b81164b491c6 "Red Orange Green" -->

### Meaning of the traffic lights
- Red means stop.
- Orange means get ready.
- Green means go.
