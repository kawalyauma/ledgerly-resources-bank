# TOPIC: TRANSPORT
## SUB-TOPIC: Road signs

### New words
- road, sign, stop, school, zebra, crossing, parking.

### Meaning of road signs
- Road signs are symbols put on roads to guide road users.
<!-- source: lchk_bc264fd781ad4bb08d611f7b2f3f85f3 "symbols put on roads to guide road users" -->

### Signs on the road
- Stop
- School ahead
- Zebra crossing
- Railway crossing
- No parking
<!-- source: lchk_0eef048d0d11478fabe8abe5478ec9b0 "School ahead Zebra crossing" -->

### Importance of road signs
- Road signs help to reduce traffic jams.
- Road signs help to reduce road accidents.
<!-- source: lchk_0eef048d0d11478fabe8abe5478ec9b0 "They help in reducing traffic jam and road accidents" -->

### Zebra crossing
- A zebra crossing is a safe place for crossing a road.
<!-- source: lchk_db5aee10b6944b13b0185638aff6c33f "Use zebra crossing, it is a safe place to cross from" -->
