# TOPIC: OUR LEADERS IN THE DISTRICT
## SUB-TOPIC: ROLES OF DISTRICT OFFICIALS

### Duties of the district chairperson
- The district chairperson coordinates local-government activities.
- The district chairperson appoints other officials.
- The district chairperson supervises local officers.
- The chairperson monitors district administration.
- District councils plan community development projects.
<!-- source: lchk_b5e07aa2f0d74d81ba74c9e3b4d442ff "Duties of the Chairperson L.C.V" -->

### Duties of the Resident District Commissioner
- The RDC represents the president in the district.
- The RDC chairs district security meetings.
- The RDC advises district chairpersons on national matters.
- The RDC coordinates central-government activities.
- The RDC helps implement central-government policies.
<!-- source: lchk_b5e07aa2f0d74d81ba74c9e3b4d442ff "Roles of the RDC" -->

### Duties of health, education and agricultural officials
- The DDHS supervises district health centres.
- The DDHS guides workers during disease outbreaks.
- The DEO oversees district education programmes.
- The DEO improves standards in district schools.
- The agricultural officer teaches farmers good farming methods.
- The veterinary officer advises livestock farmers.
<!-- source: lchk_373ac6ec3b7042c482fab204c740d6db "District Director of Health services (DDHS)" -->

### Duties of other district officers
- The district police commander maintains law and order.
- The district water officer distributes clean water.
- The forestry officer encourages planting of trees.
- The fisheries officer fights illegal fishing.
- The district engineer helps build roads and bridges.
- The district sports officer manages sports activities.
