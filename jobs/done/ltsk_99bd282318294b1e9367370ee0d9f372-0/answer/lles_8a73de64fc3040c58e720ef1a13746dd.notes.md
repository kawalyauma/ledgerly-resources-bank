# TOPIC: THE GOVERNMENT OF UGANDA
## SUB-TOPIC: Human rights

### Meaning of human rights
**Human rights** are the basic freedoms that belong to people because they are human beings. They apply to everyone, regardless of differences such as language, origin, religion or sex. The notes describe rights as freedoms and protections that people should enjoy. These rights are important for safety, dignity and fair treatment. Government has a responsibility to protect the rights of people. Citizens also have duties to respect the rights of others and help prevent abuse in their communities.

### Examples of human rights
Examples of fundamental human rights in the P5 sources include the **right to life**, the **right to education**, freedom of worship, freedom of movement, freedom of expression and the right to a fair hearing in court. Other examples include owning property, freedom from discrimination and protection from torture. These rights protect different parts of people's lives. For example, education helps children learn, while a fair hearing provides a person with an opportunity to be heard in court. Rights should be respected by individuals and public authorities.
<!-- source: lchk_6dd64f60202a49f7807c72cd6775b286 "Examples of fundamental human rights" -->

### Human rights abuse
**Human rights abuse** is the violation or denial of a person's basic rights. The learning notes name examples such as unfair detention, discrimination, torture, denial of freedom of movement and lack of a fair hearing. Abuse may happen when someone is treated unfairly or prevented from enjoying a right. It can affect people's safety, freedom and welfare. Reporting cases of abuse and teaching people about their rights can help protect communities. Citizens should avoid violating the freedoms of others.
<!-- source: lchk_34a5d4a3b16d47f5967a8c7c879ba1ab "Human Rights abuse" -->

### Protection of human rights
The **Uganda Human Rights Commission (UHRC)** helps promote and protect human rights in Uganda. The notes identify its functions as teaching citizens about rights, investigating complaints and receiving reports of violations. Government can protect people by enforcing laws against abuse and maintaining peace. Citizens can help by respecting other people's rights, reporting abuse and explaining rights to others. These actions support fairness and make people more aware of how they should treat one another. Human rights must be protected for all people.
