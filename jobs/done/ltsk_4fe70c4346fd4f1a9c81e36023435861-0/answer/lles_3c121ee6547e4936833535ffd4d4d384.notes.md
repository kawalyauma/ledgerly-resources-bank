# TOPIC: DISCIPLESHIP AND ITS REWARDS
## SUB-TOPIC: WEEK 3 REVIEW

### Summary: Jesus' teaching on discipleship
- A disciple is a follower of Jesus Christ.
- Discipleship involves imitating Jesus' examples.
- Disciples should live upright lives.
- Christians should be humble before God.
- Christians should work for peace.
- Christians should remain brave during persecution.
- Obedience is part of Christian discipleship.
- Christians should forgive those who offend them.
- St Francis gave his property to the poor.
- Khama preached against murder and witchcraft.
- Nicky Cruz changed his life after becoming Christian.

### Summary: Finding happiness in the Beatitudes
- Jesus taught the Beatitudes in Matthew chapter five.
- The spiritually poor are promised the kingdom of heaven.
- God promises comfort to those who mourn.
- God promises fulfilment to people who are humble.
- God shows mercy to merciful people.
- Those who seek righteousness will be satisfied.
- Pure-hearted people are promised they will see God.
- Peacemakers will be called God's children.
- Those persecuted for righteousness are promised heaven.
- Disciples should follow Jesus' teaching about humility.
- Christians should show mercy and work for peace.
