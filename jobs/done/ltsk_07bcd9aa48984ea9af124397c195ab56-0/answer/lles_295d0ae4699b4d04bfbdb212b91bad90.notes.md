# TOPIC: FRIENDS ON THE WAY
## SUB-TOPIC: MEANING AND IMPORTANCE OF FRIENDSHIP

### Meaning of friendship
<!-- source: lchk_78d372dc30cb4cb89f47069270c69285 "Friendship is the relationship between friends" -->
- **Friend** means a person with whom someone relates freely and openly.
- **Friendship** means a relationship between people who love or like one another.
- Friends may share their feelings, ideas and personal secrets in trust.
- True friends take time to understand one another's character and weaknesses.
- Friends can support each other when their situations become difficult.
- A friendship needs mutual care, kindness, trust and respect to grow.

### Importance of friendship
<!-- source: lchk_d48c200e56db46249636488659495593 "Importance of friendship" -->
- Friends give us good advice when we face important decisions.
- Friends encourage us when we experience challenges, worry or discouragement.
- Friends give practical support during sickness and times of need.
- Friendship creates happiness because people feel loved and accepted.
- Friends help one another to feel secure and confident.
- Friends provide company and help us feel that we belong.
- Friends share ideas, plans and secrets with one another.
- Friendship helps people discover their abilities and personal strengths.
- Friends support one another in times of both happiness and trouble.

### Jesus as a true friend
<!-- source: lchk_d48c200e56db46249636488659495593 "Jesus as a friend" -->
- Jesus commanded his disciples to love one another as he loved them.
- John 15:12–15 teaches Christians to follow Jesus' example of love.
- Jesus served others rather than seeking personal comfort and advantage.
- He washed his disciples' feet as an example of humble service.
- He helped people to meet their physical and spiritual needs.
- He gave his life for others, showing selfless and sacrificial love.
- He spent time with those needing comfort, care and compassion.
- Jesus taught people to love their neighbours as themselves.

### Note
<!-- source: lchk_51b55a93331c44b2adf7b9bd06af1c4e "Qualities of a good friend" -->
- Strong friendship includes trust, humility, kindness, patience and loyalty.
- True friendship involves support during difficult times, not happiness alone.
- Christians are encouraged to follow Jesus' example of loving service.
