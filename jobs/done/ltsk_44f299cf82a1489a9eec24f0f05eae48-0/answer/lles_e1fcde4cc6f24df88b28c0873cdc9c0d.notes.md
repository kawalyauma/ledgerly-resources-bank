# TOPIC: THE GOVERNMENT OF UGANDA
## SUB-TOPIC: Practice: the three organs of government

### Remember
1. The **Executive** administers government work and is headed by the President.
2. The **Legislature** makes laws and is headed by the Speaker of Parliament.
3. The **Judiciary** interprets laws and is headed by the Chief Justice.
4. Members of the Executive include the President, ministers and civil servants.
5. Parliament includes constituency MPs, women representatives and representatives of special groups.
6. The Judiciary includes judges, justices, magistrates, attorneys and lawyers.
7. The **Supreme Court** is the highest court and is headed by the Chief Justice.
8. Courts settle disputes, punish law breakers and protect the rights of citizens.
