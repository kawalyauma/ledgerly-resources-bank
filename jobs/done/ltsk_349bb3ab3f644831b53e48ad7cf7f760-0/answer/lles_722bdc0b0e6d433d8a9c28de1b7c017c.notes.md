# TOPIC: COMMUNICABLE INTESTINAL DISEASES
## SUB-TOPIC: THE FOUR FS OF DISEASE SPREAD

### The four Fs
- The four Fs explain a common way diarrhoeal germs spread.
- The first F stands for faeces.
- The second F stands for flies.
- The third F stands for food.
- The fourth F stands for fingers.
- These pathways can transfer germs into human bodies.
<!-- source: lchk_5dd0cd1e05894169bf81849f5b76f1e7 "Faeces Flies Food Fingers in that order." -->

### Germs from faeces to food
- Faeces from infected people can contain germs.
- Houseflies may land on infected faeces.
- Flies then move from dirty places to food.
- Germs can be left on uncovered food.
- Eating that food may cause diarrhoeal disease.
<!-- source: lchk_02bfe390241e46b39ce1b6952a30da0f "Flies pick the faeces with germs and carry them to uncovered food." -->

### Preventing the four Fs pathway
- Dispose of faeces in latrines.
- Wash hands after using a latrine.
- Cover cooked food against houseflies.
- Keep household surroundings clean.
- Keep water sources free from human waste.
- Remove places where houseflies breed.
