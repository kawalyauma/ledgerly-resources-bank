# TOPIC: COMMUNICABLE INTESTINAL DISEASES
## SUB-TOPIC: CHOLERA AND ITS SPREAD

### Meaning and cause
- Cholera is a communicable diarrhoeal disease.
- Cholera is caused by bacteria.
- The bacterium is called Vibrio cholerae.
- Cholera germs can be found in contaminated water.
- Cholera is one of the intestinal diseases.
<!-- source: lchk_e937545f9b434bf1ab2831c77a8d934b "Cholera is diarrhoeal disease caused by bacteria known as vibrio cholerae." -->

### How cholera spreads
- Cholera germs may contaminate drinking water.
- Infected faeces can contain cholera germs.
- Houseflies can carry germs from faeces to food.
- Dirty hands can put germs on food.
- Eating contaminated food can cause illness.
<!-- source: lchk_723989131cb744f3969ba6b0d2e0b985 "How cholera is spread" -->

### Signs and effects
- Cholera may cause severe watery diarrhoea.
- Vomiting can occur in cholera patients.
- Diarrhoea and vomiting cause serious water loss.
- Patients may become dehydrated.
- Loss of body water is dangerous.
<!-- source: lchk_e937545f9b434bf1ab2831c77a8d934b "Signs and symptoms of cholera" -->

### Preventing cholera
- Use clean and safe drinking water.
- Wash hands before handling food.
- Dispose of faeces in toilets or pit latrines.
- Cover food against houseflies.
- Keep water sources and surroundings clean.
