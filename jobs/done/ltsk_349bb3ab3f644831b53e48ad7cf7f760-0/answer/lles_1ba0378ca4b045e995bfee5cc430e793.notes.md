# TOPIC: COMMUNICABLE INTESTINAL DISEASES
## SUB-TOPIC: CAUSES OF DIARRHOEA

### Meaning of diarrhoea
- Diarrhoea means frequent passing of watery stool.
- Diarrhoea is a communicable intestinal disease.
- Bacteria and viruses can cause diarrhoea.
- Diarrhoea can result from contaminated food or water.
<!-- source: lchk_02bfe390241e46b39ce1b6952a30da0f "Diarrhoea is the frequent passing out of watery stool." -->

### How diarrhoea spreads
- Infected faeces contain disease-causing germs.
- Houseflies may transfer germs from faeces to food.
- Germs can enter the body through contaminated food.
- Dirty hands may carry germs onto food.
- Unsafe drinking water may contain harmful germs.
<!-- source: lchk_02bfe390241e46b39ce1b6952a30da0f "How diarrhoea is spread" -->

### Dehydration
- Severe diarrhoea can cause dehydration.
- Dehydration occurs when the body loses too much water.
- Dehydrated patients need water and mineral salts.
- Severe dehydration can weaken a sick person.
- Diarrhoea can cause a person to lose body fluids.
<!-- source: lchk_02bfe390241e46b39ce1b6952a30da0f "Dehydration" -->

### Preventing diarrhoeal diseases
- Drink safe and clean water.
- Wash hands before handling food.
- Use a pit latrine for faeces.
- Keep food covered against houseflies.
- Maintain sanitation around home and school.
