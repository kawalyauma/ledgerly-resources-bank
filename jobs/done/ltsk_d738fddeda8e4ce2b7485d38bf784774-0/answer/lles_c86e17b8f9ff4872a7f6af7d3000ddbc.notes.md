# TOPIC: READING FROM THE QUR'AN — SURAT AN-NAS
## SUB-TOPIC: Reciting Surat An-Nas

### Surat An-Nas
- Surat An-Nas is a chapter of the Holy Qur’an.
- Surat An-Nas has six verses.
- The surah is recited with correct articulation.
<!-- source: lchk_8f89ee9db7564a2c8b613fec316bfb27 "Surah (chapter) An-Nas" -->

### Verses of Surat An-Nas
- Qul a'oozu birabbin naas
- Malikin naas
- Ilaahin naas
- Min sharril waswaasil khannaas
- Allazee yuwaswisu fee sudoorin naas
- Minal jinnati wannaas
<!-- source: lchk_8f89ee9db7564a2c8b613fec316bfb27 "Qul a'oozu birabbin naas" -->
