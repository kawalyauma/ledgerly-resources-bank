## SUB-TOPIC: ORGANS OF GOVERNMENT PART B

### Remember
- It implements laws. Groups of people that make up the executive organ
- To provide social services People who belongs to the Executive
- Legislature is the national assembly of a country.
- Legislature is the law-making body in Uganda.
- Before Uganda became independent, the assembly was called the legislature council
- It is headed by the parliamentary speaker. People who belongs to the legislature
- To give advice to the executive
- To protect the national constitution Legislature is headed by the speaker of parliament. Important personalities in parliament.
