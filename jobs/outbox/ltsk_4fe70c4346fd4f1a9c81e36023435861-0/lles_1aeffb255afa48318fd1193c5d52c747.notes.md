# TOPIC: DISCIPLESHIP AND ITS REWARDS
## SUB-TOPIC: FINDING HAPPINESS: THE BEATITUDES

### Jesus' teaching about happiness
- Jesus taught his disciples about happiness in Matthew 5:1–11.
- Jesus called blessed the people who live by God's will.
- The Beatitudes teach humility, mercy and peacemaking.
- People should desire to do what God requires.
- Disciples should copy the character and example of Jesus.
<!-- source: lchk_b7a765d26a7d4b2a803fec3f9aa4ef7f "This is what Jesus taught about discipleship. (Matthew 5:1 – 11)" -->

### People who receive the kingdom of heaven
- The spiritually poor are promised the kingdom of heaven.
- Those who seek God should recognise their spiritual needs.
- People persecuted for righteousness are promised God's kingdom.
- Followers of Jesus should show courage during persecution.
- Disciples should remain obedient when facing difficulties.
<!-- source: lchk_526fa31e33c048179dbdaa358f860e49 "Happy are those who know that they are spiritually poor!" -->

### Promises to the humble and merciful
- God will comfort people who mourn.
- Humble people will receive what God has promised.
- God will show mercy to people who show mercy.
- Those desiring righteousness will be fully satisfied.
- Pure-hearted people are promised that they will see God.
- People who work for peace will be called God's children.
<!-- source: lchk_526fa31e33c048179dbdaa358f860e49 "Happy are those who are pure in heart! They will see God." -->

### Lessons Christians learn from the Beatitudes
- Christians should be merciful to other people.
- Christians should make peace with their neighbours.
- Christians should remain humble before God.
- Christians should seek to do what God requires.
- Christians should show courage during persecution.
- Forgiveness and obedience are examples of true discipleship.
- Christians should not abandon their faith during suffering.
