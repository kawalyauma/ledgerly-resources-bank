# TOPIC: TRANSPORT
## SUB-TOPIC: Week 1 review

### New words
- transport, road, water, railway, air, train, pilot.

### Summary: meaning and types of transport
- Transport moves people and goods from one place to another.
- Types of transport: road, water, railway and air.
<!-- source: lchk_933da9abb9964b45a40353e49b80f960 "There are four types of transport" -->

### Summary: water transport
- Boats and ships move on water.
- A captain operates a ship; a sailor sails a boat.
<!-- source: lchk_933da9abb9964b45a40353e49b80f960 "Captain – operates a ship. Sailor – sails a boat" -->

### Summary: railway transport
- A train moves on rails.
- Trains are found at a railway station.
<!-- source: lchk_0eef048d0d11478fabe8abe5478ec9b0 "Train – railway station" -->

### Summary: air transport
- Aeroplanes and helicopters are means of air transport.
- A pilot flies an aeroplane.
- People travelling in an aeroplane are passengers.
<!-- source: lchk_bc264fd781ad4bb08d611f7b2f3f85f3 "People who travel in an aeroplane are passengers" -->
