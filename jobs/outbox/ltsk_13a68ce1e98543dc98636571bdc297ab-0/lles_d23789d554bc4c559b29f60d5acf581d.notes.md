# TOPIC: TRANSPORT
## SUB-TOPIC: Air transport

### New words
- air, aeroplane, helicopter, pilot, passengers, airport.

### Meaning of air transport
- Air transport is the movement of people and goods above land and water.
<!-- source: lchk_0eef048d0d11478fabe8abe5478ec9b0 "Movement of people with their goods above water and land" -->

### Means of air transport
- Aeroplanes
- Helicopters
<!-- source: lchk_bc264fd781ad4bb08d611f7b2f3f85f3 "Aeroplanes Helicopters" -->

### People who use air transport
- A pilot flies an aeroplane.
- People travelling in an aeroplane are passengers.
<!-- source: lchk_bc264fd781ad4bb08d611f7b2f3f85f3 "People who travel in an aeroplane are passengers" -->

### Places where we find aeroplanes
- Aeroplanes are found at airports and airstrips.
<!-- source: lchk_0eef048d0d11478fabe8abe5478ec9b0 "Aeroplanes – airports/ air strips" -->
