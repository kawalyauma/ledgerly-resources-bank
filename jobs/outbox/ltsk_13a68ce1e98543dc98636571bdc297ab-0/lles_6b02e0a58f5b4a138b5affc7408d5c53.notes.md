# TOPIC: TRANSPORT
## SUB-TOPIC: Water transport

### New words
- water, boat, ship, canoe, ferry, captain, sailor.

### Meaning of water transport
- Water transport is the movement of people and goods on water.
<!-- source: lchk_933da9abb9964b45a40353e49b80f960 "movement of people with their goods on water" -->

### Means of water transport
- Boats
- Ferries
- Canoes
- Ships
<!-- source: lchk_933da9abb9964b45a40353e49b80f960 "Boats, ferries, yacht, canoes, ships" -->

### People who use water transport
- A captain operates a ship.
- A sailor sails a boat.
<!-- source: lchk_933da9abb9964b45a40353e49b80f960 "Captain – operates a ship. Sailor – sails a boat" -->

### Problems on water
- Bad weather
- Dangerous animals, such as crocodiles
<!-- source: lchk_933da9abb9964b45a40353e49b80f960 "Bad weather Dangerous animals e.g. crocodiles" -->
