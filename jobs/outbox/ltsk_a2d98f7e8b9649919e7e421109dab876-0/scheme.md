## Curriculum themes
- Weeks 1–2: **History of Islam — Khadijah and the Revelation**.
- Weeks 2–3: **Reading of the Qur'an — Rites of the Qur'an**.
- Weeks 4–5: **Tawhiid (Faith) — Uniqueness of the Qur'an**.
- Weeks 5–6: **Fiqh (Practice) — Impurities and Toilet Manners**.
- Weeks 7–9: **Moral and Spiritual Teachings — Dua for Knowledge**.
- Weeks 10–10: **History of Islam — The Call**.

## Gaps
- NCDC allocates Weeks 10–12 to The Call. This ten-week term supports only the beginning and first recipients of the Prophet's call. Public preaching, reactions and problems faced need additional sourced periods.
- The final period is reserved for end-of-term assessment. The full questions, answers and marking guide must be selected from the resource pack when the lesson is written.
- Ledgerly reserves the third period each week for revision. No unsupported review questions are invented here.
