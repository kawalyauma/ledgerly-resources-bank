# TOPIC: COMMUNICABLE INTESTINAL DISEASES
## SUB-TOPIC: DYSENTERY AND ITS CAUSES

### Meaning and causes
- Dysentery is the passing of watery stool containing blood.
- Bacillary dysentery is caused by shigella bacteria.
- Amoebic dysentery is caused by amoeba.
- Dysentery is a communicable intestinal disease.
- Infected faeces may carry dysentery germs.
<!-- source: lchk_e937545f9b434bf1ab2831c77a8d934b "Dysentery is the passing out of watery faeces with blood." -->

### How dysentery spreads
- Houseflies may carry dysentery germs.
- Flies can carry germs onto uncovered food.
- Eating contaminated food spreads intestinal infections.
- Infected faeces can pollute water sources.
- Dirty hands may transfer germs to food.
<!-- source: lchk_5dd0cd1e05894169bf81849f5b76f1e7 "How is dysentery spread" -->

### Signs and symptoms
- Bloody diarrhoea is a sign of dysentery.
- A sick person can develop severe diarrhoea.
- Dysentery may cause dehydration.
- An infected person loses body water.
- A sick person may suffer abdominal discomfort.
<!-- source: lchk_e937545f9b434bf1ab2831c77a8d934b "Signs and symptoms of dysentery" -->

### Ways of preventing dysentery
- Dispose of human faeces in latrines.
- Wash hands with soap and water.
- Keep food and drinking water safe.
- Cover food against houseflies.
- Destroy housefly breeding places.
