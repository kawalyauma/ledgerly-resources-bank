# TOPIC: THE GOVERNMENT OF UGANDA
## SUB-TOPIC: Practice: rights and responsibilities of citizens and child labour

### Remember
1. **Citizenship** is the legal right to belong to a country.
2. A good citizen obeys laws, respects rights and pays taxes when required.
3. Citizenship may be acquired through birth, descent, registration, naturalisation or adoption.
4. **Human rights** are freedoms to which all people are entitled.
5. Examples of rights include life, education, worship and a fair hearing.
6. **Child abuse** violates children's rights and can harm their wellbeing.
7. **Child labour** is the employment of children in heavy or harmful work.
8. Education, child protection and enforcing laws help reduce child abuse and labour.
