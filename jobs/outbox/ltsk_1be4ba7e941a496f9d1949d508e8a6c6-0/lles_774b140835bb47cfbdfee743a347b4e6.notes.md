# TOPIC: LENGTH, MASS AND CAPACITY
## SUB-TOPIC: Comparing areas: finding a base

### New words
- equal area, base, height, equation.

### Comparing areas
- Equal areas may have different bases and heights.
- $\frac12 b_1h_1=\frac12 b_2h_2$.
- $\text{Base}=2\times\text{area}\div\text{height}$.
<!-- source: lchk_6a71c8bb4c5c46c59a2956830a6a01d9 "two triangles occupying the same area" -->

### Example: two bases of one triangle
- Triangle ABD has base $BD=12\text{ cm}$ and height $AC=10\text{ cm}$.
- Its height to base AD is $BE=8\text{ cm}$. Find AD.
- $\frac12\times AD\times8=\frac12\times12\times10$.
- $4AD=60$.
- $AD=60\div4=15\text{ cm}$.
<!-- source: lchk_35e370d976c340a69e206bdcaf99d939 "Find the length of AD" -->

### Example: base from a known area
- Find the base of a triangle with area $60\text{ cm}^2$ and height $12\text{ cm}$.
- $\frac12\times b\times12=60$.
- $6b=60$.
- $b=60\div6=10\text{ cm}$.
<!-- source: lchk_35e370d976c340a69e206bdcaf99d939 "Find the base of a triangle whose area is 60cm 2" -->

### Example: base from twenty-four square centimetres
- A triangle has area $24\text{ cm}^2$ and height $6\text{ cm}$. Find its base.
- $\frac12\times b\times6=24$.
- $3b=24$.
- $b=24\div3=8\text{ cm}$.
