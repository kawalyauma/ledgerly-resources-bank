# TOPIC: LENGTH, MASS AND CAPACITY
## SUB-TOPIC: Area of a trapezium

### New words
- trapezium, parallel sides, perpendicular height, area.

### Area of a trapezium
- A trapezium has two parallel sides.
- $A=\frac12h(a+b)$.
- $a$ and $b$ are the parallel sides.
- $h$ is the perpendicular height.
<!-- source: lchk_22932c39e53641f59019ad7af8ce184b "Area of trapezium" -->

### Example: parallel sides eight and ten centimetres
- Parallel sides are $8\text{ cm}$ and $10\text{ cm}$; height is $7\text{ cm}$.
- $A=\frac12\times7(8+10)$.
- $A=\frac12\times7\times18$.
- $A=7\times9=63\text{ cm}^2$.
<!-- source: lchk_35e370d976c340a69e206bdcaf99d939 "Area = ½ h (a + b)" -->

### Example: parallel sides seven and ten centimetres
- Parallel sides are $7\text{ cm}$ and $10\text{ cm}$; height is $4\text{ cm}$.
- $A=\frac12\times4(7+10)$.
- $A=2\times17=34\text{ cm}^2$.
<!-- source: lchk_22932c39e53641f59019ad7af8ce184b "A = ½ x 4cm (7cm + 10cm)" -->

### Example: finding the second parallel side
- Area is $60\text{ cm}^2$, height is $4\text{ cm}$ and one parallel side is $10\text{ cm}$.
- $\frac12\times4(a+10)=60$.
- $2(a+10)=60$.
- $2a+20=60$.
- $2a=40$.
- $a=20\text{ cm}$.
