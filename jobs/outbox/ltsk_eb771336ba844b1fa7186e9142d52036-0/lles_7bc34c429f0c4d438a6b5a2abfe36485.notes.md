# TOPIC: TRANSPORT
## SUB-TOPIC: Week 2 review

### New words
- road, sign, traffic, lights, safe, footpath.

### Summary: road transport
- Road transport moves people and goods on land.
- Buses, cars, lorries and bicycles use roads.
<!-- source: lchk_933da9abb9964b45a40353e49b80f960 "movement of people with their goods on land" -->

### Summary: road signs
- Road signs guide road users.
- A zebra crossing is a safe place to cross a road.
<!-- source: lchk_db5aee10b6944b13b0185638aff6c33f "Use zebra crossing, it is a safe place to cross from" -->

### Summary: traffic lights
- Red means stop; orange means get ready; green means go.
<!-- source: lchk_db5aee10b6944b13b0185638aff6c33f "Red – stop Orange – get ready Green – go" -->

### Summary: safe road use
- Obey traffic rules.
- Look right, left and right again before crossing.
- Use footpaths.
- Do not run while crossing a road.
<!-- source: lchk_db5aee10b6944b13b0185638aff6c33f "Look right, look left and look right again" -->
