### Features and related facts about local government
- Local government is under the ministry of local government
- The highest local government unit is LC5
- Central government is one government while local government has many local government
- Central government runs major hospitals while local government controls dispensaries and health centres.
- Central government builds major roads while local government maintains feeder roads
- This is a law made by local council.
- The vice chairperson is responsible for children affairs on the local council1 committee
- Local election. This is a formal democratic process in which people choose leaders at local council level ie. Local council I -V leaders.
- The Local government (LC1-LCV).
