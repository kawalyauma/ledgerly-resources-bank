Source-led weekly scheme: one lesson is 40 minutes. Ledgerly reserves the third lesson each week for review.

## NCDC subthemes
- Weeks 1–3: Forgiveness and Acceptance
- Weeks 4–6: Treating Others Fairly
- Weeks 7–9: Jesus Brings Joy
- Weeks 10–10: The Joy of Christmas


## Sourcing and gaps
- The NCDC Theme 'The Joy of Christmas' extends to Weeks 10–12; this school's ten-week term permits only the first week of the theme. Remaining Christmas-play activities require further pacing or review allocation.
- The last lesson is reserved for an end-of-term assessment using source questions. The full assessment paper and answer key must be selected from the pack when the lesson is written.
