# TOPIC: EXPRESSION OF THE FUTURE
## SUB-TOPIC: Using since

### New words
- since, starting point, duration, present perfect.

### Since and for
- Since introduces the point of time when an action began.
- For introduces the length of time an action has lasted.
- A starting point and a duration give different time information.
<!-- source: lchk_52d32128a6914ca8ae03917f25c7b78f "Since is used when a point of time from which an action began is given" -->

### Example 1
- The boy has been crying since 9:00 a.m.
- The starting point is 9:00 a.m.
- The boy’s crying began at that time.
<!-- source: lchk_52d32128a6914ca8ae03917f25c7b78f "The boy has been crying since 9:00am" -->

### Example 2
- The baby has cried for an hour.
- An hour is a length of time.
- The sentence gives a duration.
<!-- source: lchk_52d32128a6914ca8ae03917f25c7b78f "The baby has cried for an hour" -->

### Example 3
- They have played for four hours.
- I have not seen my mother for two years.
- Four hours and two years are durations.
<!-- source: lchk_52d32128a6914ca8ae03917f25c7b78f "They have played for four hours" -->
