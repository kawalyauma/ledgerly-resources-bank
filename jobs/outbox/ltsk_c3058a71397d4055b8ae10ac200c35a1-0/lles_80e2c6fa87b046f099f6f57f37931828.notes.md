# TOPIC: EXPRESSION OF THE FUTURE
## SUB-TOPIC: Using will and shall

### New words
- will, shall, shall not, will not, future.

### Will and shall
- I / We: shall; shall not.
- She / They: will; will not.
- play with my friends.
- go with him.
- go on holiday.
- swim in the lake.
- disturb you today.

### Example 1
- We shall not play with him.
- We shall go with him.
<!-- source: lchk_7685308ccbde482cb6da1bdbb89736ea "For example" -->

### Example 2
- Jane will go to school tomorrow.
- We shall not go to the market next week.
- We shall not see elephants because they are not in the zoo.
