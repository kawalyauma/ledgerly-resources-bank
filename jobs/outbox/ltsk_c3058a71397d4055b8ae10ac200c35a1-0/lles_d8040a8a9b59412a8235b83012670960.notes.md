# TOPIC: EXPRESSION OF THE FUTURE
## SUB-TOPIC: Future-time vocabulary

### New words
- tomorrow, next week, next month, next year, shan’t, won’t.

### Future-time words
- tomorrow
- next week
- next month
- next year
<!-- source: lchk_7685308ccbde482cb6da1bdbb89736ea "tomorrow 5. shall not" -->

### Negative forms
- shall not
- will not
- shan’t
- won’t
<!-- source: lchk_7685308ccbde482cb6da1bdbb89736ea "next month 7. shan’t" -->

### Example 1
- Jane will go to school tomorrow.
- We shall not go to the market next week.
<!-- source: lchk_f716af72c8cc4a5a82acd048b8952822 "Jane will go to school tomorrow" -->

### Example 2
- We shall not play with him.
- We shall go with him.
- We shall see lions, chimpanzees and monkeys.
- We shall come back home in the evening.
<!-- source: lchk_7685308ccbde482cb6da1bdbb89736ea "We shall not play with him" -->
