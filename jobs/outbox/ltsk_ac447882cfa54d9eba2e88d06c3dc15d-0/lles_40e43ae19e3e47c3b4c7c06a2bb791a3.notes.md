# TOPIC: THE GOVERNMENT OF UGANDA
## SUB-TOPIC: The president, cabinet, ministers and civil servants

### The President
The **President** is the political head of Uganda and heads the Executive. The President is the head of state and government and is commander-in-chief of the armed forces. The President represents Uganda internationally and chairs Cabinet meetings. The President also appoints leading government officials, including ministers, who are approved by Parliament. Other responsibilities in the supplied notes include planning national development, approving parliamentary bills and granting mercy to deserving offenders. These responsibilities show the President's leadership role in the Executive.
<!-- source: lchk_34af8a516be44e0ba5c1a92a22a56fc8 "He represents the country internationally." -->

### The Cabinet
The **Cabinet** is made up of government ministers working under the President. The President chairs Cabinet meetings. Cabinet ministers consider programmes and activities to be carried out by the civil service. These programmes help different government offices carry out their work. Cabinet therefore helps guide government business while civil servants implement the programmes in their offices. The Cabinet, ministers and President belong to the Executive, but they perform different parts of its administrative work.
<!-- source: lchk_37455fa779f74da3964836e8dbdbb4b7 "The cabinet ministers suggest the programmes and activities to be carried out by the civil service." -->

### Ministers
A **minister** is the political head of a ministry. Ministers are appointed by the President and approved by Parliament. Their duty is to make sure their ministries implement government policies. Cabinet ministers are assisted by ministers of state in their work. The **Prime Minister** leads government business and is described as the chief minister in the supplied notes. Government has ministries responsible for such areas as education, health and public service. Ministers guide their ministries as part of the Executive.
<!-- source: lchk_34af8a516be44e0ba5c1a92a22a56fc8 "The minister is the title given to the head of a ministry." -->

### Civil servants
**Civil servants** are people employed by government to carry out daily public work. Examples include government teachers, doctors, nurses and engineers. They implement government programmes and provide services to citizens. The highest civil servant in a ministry is the **Permanent Secretary**. At district level, the head of civil servants is the **Chief Administrative Officer (CAO)**. The Public Service Commission is responsible for recruiting government workers. Civil servants continue the routine work of administration and help government programmes reach communities.
