# TOPIC: OUR LEADERS IN THE DISTRICT
## SUB-TOPIC: DISTRICT POLITICAL AND CIVIC HEADS

### Political heads of local government
- Political leaders are chosen through elections.
- The district political head is Chairperson LC V.
- The municipal political head is a mayor.
- The sub-county head is Chairperson LC III.
- The district speaker chairs district council meetings.
<!-- source: lchk_b5e07aa2f0d74d81ba74c9e3b4d442ff "In municipalities, the political leader is a mayor." -->

### Resident District Commissioner
- The RDC represents the president in the district.
- The president appoints the Resident District Commissioner.
- The RDC chairs the district security committee.
- The RDC coordinates central-government activities in the district.
- The RDC advises the district chairperson on national matters.
<!-- source: lchk_b5e07aa2f0d74d81ba74c9e3b4d442ff "Resident District Commissioner (RDC)" -->

### Civic leaders and their duties
- Civic leaders provide social services to communities.
- Civic leaders obtain office through appointment.
- The CAO heads district civil servants.
- The DDHS supervises health centres and workers.
- The DEO improves standards of education.
- The veterinary officer advises livestock farmers.
- The agricultural officer advises crop farmers.
- The district water officer provides clean water.
- The district police commander maintains law and order.
