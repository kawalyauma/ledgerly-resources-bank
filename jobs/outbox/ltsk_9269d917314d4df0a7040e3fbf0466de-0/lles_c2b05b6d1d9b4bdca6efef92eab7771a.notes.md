# TOPIC: OUR LEADERS IN THE DISTRICT
## SUB-TOPIC: DECENTRALISATION

### Meaning of decentralisation
- Decentralisation transfers some powers from central to local government.
- Central government serves the whole nation.
- Local governments provide services in local areas.
- Uganda has central and local governments.
<!-- source: lchk_27d06babc4484d549057f8cd882b4c53 "This is the transfer of some powers from the central government to the local" -->

### Differences between central and local government
- Uganda has one central government and many local governments.
- Central government maintains major roads.
- Local government maintains feeder roads.
- Central government runs major hospitals.
- Local government controls health centres.
- Central government controls all armed forces.
- Local government controls local defence forces.
<!-- source: lchk_27d06babc4484d549057f8cd882b4c53 "Difference between the Central government and Local government." -->

### Levels of local government
- A village is served by Local Council One.
- A parish is served by Local Council Two.
- A sub-county is served by Local Council Three.
- A district is served by Local Council Five.
<!-- source: lchk_27d06babc4484d549057f8cd882b4c53 "Levels of local government." -->

### Advantages of decentralisation
- Decentralisation brings services nearer to people.
- It makes government administration easier.
- It promotes development in different areas.
- It creates job opportunities for people.
<!-- source: lchk_27d06babc4484d549057f8cd882b4c53 "Advantages of decentralisation." -->
