# TOPIC: TIME: USING STUDY TIME
## SUB-TOPIC: Reading: Mukaga’s daily plan

### New words
- timetable, restaurant, photographs, vehicle, lunch.

### Mukaga’s plan: morning
- 8:00 a.m.: Breakfast at Doso’s restaurant.
- 8:30 a.m.: Board a vehicle to Entebbe with friends.
- 10:30 a.m.: Get a special-hire taxi to the zoo.
- 11:00 a.m.: Look at different animals and birds.

### Mukaga’s plan: afternoon
- 12:30 p.m.: Take photographs at the zoo.
- 1:00 p.m.: Have lunch with friends.
- 2:00 p.m.: Swim with friends.
- 2:45 p.m.: Board a vehicle back to Jinja.
