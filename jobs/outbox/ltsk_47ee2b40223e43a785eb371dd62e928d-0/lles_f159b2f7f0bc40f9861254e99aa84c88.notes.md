# TOPIC: TIME: KEEPING A DIARY
## SUB-TOPIC: Reading: Amanya’s diary

### New words
- diary, date, calendar, month, year.

### Diary words
- Diary: a book for keeping records.
- Date: the number of the day, month and year.
- Calendar: a list of days, weeks and months of a year.
<!-- source: lchk_b99ac999f1134b72b200041291806709 "a book for keeping records" -->

### Amanya’s diary: dates and events
- 1st: Wrote an invitation card to uncle.
- 3rd: Sent the invitation card to uncle.
- 11th: Went to the village for the birthday party.
- 12th: Father bought a new party dress.
- 14th: Aunt Monica baked the birthday cake.
- 16th: Celebrated the ninth birthday.
- 26th: Attended thanksgiving service.
- 30th: Returned home.
