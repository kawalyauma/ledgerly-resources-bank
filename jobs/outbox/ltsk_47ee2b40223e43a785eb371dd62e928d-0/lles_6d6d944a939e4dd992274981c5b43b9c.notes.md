# TOPIC: TIME: READING A WORK PLAN
## SUB-TOPIC: Reading: Namata’s work plan

### New words
- work plan, kennel, novel, uniform, rabbits.

### Namata’s work plan: Sunday to Wednesday
- Sunday: She will go to church.
- Sunday: She will feed the rabbits at 10:00 a.m.
- Monday: She will clean the kennel.
- Tuesday: She will read a novel.
- Wednesday: She will go for P.E. at school.
