# TOPIC: THE GOVERNMENT OF UGANDA
## SUB-TOPIC: The legislature

### Duties of the legislature
- To discuss and pass laws.
- To advise the executive.
- To approve the national budget.
- To approve newly appointed ministers.
- To protect the national constitution.

### Important parliamentary officers
- The Speaker of Parliament
- The Sergeant-at-Arms
- The Clerk to Parliament
<!-- source: lchk_a068c36f55a64155a9a2517aace9832f "Important personalities in parliament." -->

### Roles of parliamentary officers
- The Speaker chairs parliamentary meetings.
- The Sergeant-at-Arms keeps order in Parliament.
- The Clerk records minutes of Parliament.
