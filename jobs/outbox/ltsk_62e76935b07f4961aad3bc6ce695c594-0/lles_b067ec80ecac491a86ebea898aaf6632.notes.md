# TOPIC: THE GOVERNMENT OF UGANDA
## SUB-TOPIC: The judiciary

### Composition of the judiciary
- Judges
- Magistrates
- Court clerks
- Lawyers
<!-- source: lchk_a068c36f55a64155a9a2517aace9832f "Composition of the judiciary" -->

### Duties of the judiciary
- To interpret the law.
- To decide if the law has been broken.
- To settle disputes.
- To punish lawbreakers.
<!-- source: lchk_a068c36f55a64155a9a2517aace9832f "Duties of the judiciary" -->

### Note
- The judiciary is headed by the Chief Justice.
