# TOPIC: THE GOVERNMENT OF UGANDA
## SUB-TOPIC: The executive

### Meaning of the executive
- The executive organ does the work of government.
<!-- source: lchk_a068c36f55a64155a9a2517aace9832f "The executive organ does the work of government." -->

### Composition of the executive
- The president
- Cabinet ministers
- Civil servants
<!-- source: lchk_a068c36f55a64155a9a2517aace9832f "Composition of the executive." -->

### Duties of the executive
- To implement the law.
- To plan for the country.
- To rule the country.
- To protect people and their property.
