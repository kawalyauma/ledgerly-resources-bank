# TOPIC: THE GOVERNMENT OF UGANDA
## SUB-TOPIC: Role of government

### Meaning of government
- Government is a group of people given power to rule a country.

### Functions of government
- To construct roads.
- To build schools.
- To build hospitals.
- To maintain roads, railways and airports.
- To extend electricity to people.
- To promote national unity.
<!-- source: lchk_6f02844a54284ef2afb2352f215e6ee3 "FUNCTIONS OF GOVERNMENT" -->

### Other functions of government
- To provide clean water to people.
- To implement the laws.
- To protect people and their property.
