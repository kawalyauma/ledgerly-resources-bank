# TOPIC: THE GOVERNMENT OF UGANDA
## SUB-TOPIC: Three organs of government

### Organs of government
- The executive
- The legislature
- The judiciary

### Duties of organs of government
- The executive implements laws.
- The legislature discusses and passes laws.
- The judiciary interprets the law.
