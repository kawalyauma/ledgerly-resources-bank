# TOPIC: POPULATION, SIZE AND DISTRIBUTION
## SUB-TOPIC: Solutions to problems of high population density

### Why action is needed
An area with many people may face shortages of homes, food, jobs and access to important services. The source texts also identify slums, poor sanitation, disease spread, crime and environmental damage. These difficulties can increase as more people depend on limited land and services. Solutions are meant to improve housing, access to jobs, supplies of food and social services. The same texts include policies aimed at reducing the rate of population growth.
<!-- source: lchk_401b8346cdcf44109b333a534b4a6b3d "It leads to development of slums." -->

### Housing and settlement
In crowded towns, **storeyed buildings** can help deal with the shortage of land for homes. Government may also resettle people from heavily populated places to areas with fewer residents. Improving services in villages and rural areas can encourage people to move away from overcrowded urban centres. The source pack suggests extending **electricity** into rural areas as another development measure. Better rural services can make settlement outside big towns more attractive.
<!-- source: lchk_a89f8a96f35c42f7a1dee9e9583a10c8 "build storeyed buildings to solve a problem of land" -->

### Jobs and food supply
Establishing **large and small industries** helps create employment opportunities. Young people can be empowered to start small-scale business projects. Modern agricultural methods can be taught to increase food production and respond to shortages. More food and jobs can reduce the pressure experienced by families living in densely settled areas. Such measures also strengthen economic activity and help communities put available resources to better use.
<!-- source: lchk_f3180c29719f4e6ba4d80288742b14d2 "Establishing large and small scale industries to create employment" -->

### Managing rapid growth
The pack lists **family planning**, **education for girls** and discouraging **early marriages** and **polygamy** as ways to control population growth. It also suggests educating families about the costs of raising many children. Government can increase spending on social and medical services in order to serve a growing population. These measures can be considered with better housing, employment and agriculture so that the needs of people are met.
