# TOPIC: OUR LEADERS IN THE DISTRICT
## SUB-TOPIC: WAYS OF ACQUIRING LEADERSHIP

### Leadership through elections
- Elections are used to choose political leaders.
- Voting enables people to select preferred leaders.
- Political leaders gain authority through elections.
- Candidates compete to be chosen as leaders.
- Voting may use secret or open election systems.
<!-- source: lchk_77d43cbdf5c14f758eae480c27c4186d "Give the meaning of an election." -->

### Leadership through appointment
- Civic leaders obtain authority through appointment.
- Appointed officials provide government and social services.
- The Chief Administrative Officer is a civic leader.
- Some district officers are appointed to public office.
- Appointed leaders help administer local programmes.
<!-- source: lchk_91654079c60d445a93db08b33a025780 "Civic leaders ▪ Through appointment" -->

### Leadership through inheritance
- Some cultural leaders inherit their authority.
- Cultural leaders organise people in kingdoms and chiefdoms.
- Kings and queens are examples of cultural leaders.
- Chiefs may lead people in chiefdoms.
- Clan leaders organise members of their clans.
<!-- source: lchk_aca105cebd564cfc9d6f6d7a838c3d68 "Cultural leaders get leadership powers through inheritance." -->

### Leadership through voluntary service
- Voluntary leaders offer services without being elected.
- They become leaders through volunteering.
- Voluntary organisations help people in need.
- Scouts and girl guides help organise functions.
- Voluntary leaders can provide first aid to others.
<!-- source: lchk_aca105cebd564cfc9d6f6d7a838c3d68 "How do voluntary leaders become leaders?" -->
