# TOPIC: THE GOVERNMENT OF UGANDA
## SUB-TOPIC: Government expenditure and the national budget

### Meaning of expenditure
**Government expenditure** is the way government spends its income or revenue. The notes say money is spent on providing services and carrying out programmes. Examples of government expenditure include building schools, providing education and medical care, paying civil servants, building and maintaining roads and paying back loans. The government also spends money on security and support for people affected by disasters. These expenditures help meet the duties of government to its citizens and keep public services operating.

### The budget and financial year
A **budget** is an estimate of income and expenditure for a particular period. The **national budget** is the country's estimate of expected income and spending for a financial year. The two main parts of the budget are income and expenditure. The **financial year** is the planning year for government spending. In Uganda, the supplied notes say that the financial year begins on **1 July** and ends on **30 June** of the next year. A budget helps government identify sources of income and plan how to use funds.

### Three types of budget
The source notes describe three types of budget.
1. A **surplus budget** occurs when income is greater than expenditure.
2. A **balanced budget** occurs when income equals expenditure.
3. A **deficit budget** occurs when expenditure is greater than income.
A surplus can encourage saving and investment. A balanced budget has equal income and spending. A deficit means that expected spending is higher than expected income, which may create debt. These categories help people understand the relationship between what is earned and what is spent.
<!-- source: lchk_308194d2a0674ce7b8a2824efae4d3b8 "(iii) Deficit budget." -->

### Importance of budgeting
A national budget helps government decide how to spend available money and keep track of expected costs. The source notes say that budgeting identifies revenue sources, helps meet important needs and reduces unnecessary spending. Problems can arise if expenditure is greater than income. Causes of deficit budgets in the notes include corruption, smuggling, poor investment and unreliable sources of income. Proposed solutions include widening the tax base, diversifying the economy and encouraging investment. Planning helps the government provide public services while considering its income.
