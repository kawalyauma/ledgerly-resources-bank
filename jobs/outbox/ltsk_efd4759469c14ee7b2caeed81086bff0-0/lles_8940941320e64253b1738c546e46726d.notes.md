# TOPIC: WITNESS — CHRIST THE LIGHT
## SUB-TOPIC: CHRIST'S LIGHT SEEN IN PEOPLE

### Christ's light reflected in daily life
<!-- source: lchk_93fa2d984d4d48f7a18355666d6c2601 "Examples of how Christians can reflect the light of Jesus to others" -->
- **Christian witness** means showing the teachings of Jesus through one's conduct.
- Christians reflect his light when they forgive those who wrong them.
- They reflect his light by helping poor and hungry people.
- Christians may care for sick people and others facing hardship.
- They can offer useful services such as teaching and healing.
- Showing kindness to children reflects Jesus' loving example.
- Loving others demonstrates the care and concern Christ taught.

### Lessons from Jesus' life
<!-- source: lchk_93fa2d984d4d48f7a18355666d6c2601 "Examples of how Jesus acted like a light to us." -->
- Jesus showed sympathy to people who were suffering.
- He forgave his enemies instead of returning harm.
- Jesus obeyed the will of God during his ministry.
- He healed the sick and fed hungry people.
- He loved children and gave them his blessings.
- Christians learn to be obedient, forgiving and helpful.
- Jesus' followers are encouraged to care for others.

### Ways of serving others
<!-- source: lchk_93fa2d984d4d48f7a18355666d6c2601 "Examples of how Christians can reflect the light of Jesus to others" -->
- Helping a hungry person is a form of Christian service.
- Helping a sick neighbour shows compassion.
- Sharing support with needy families is an act of love.
- Forgiving a person who has wronged us promotes peace.
- Teaching people can be a useful service to the community.
- Christians can cooperate in activities that help others.
- Following Jesus' example requires both loving words and useful actions.
