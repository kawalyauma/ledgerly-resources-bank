# TOPIC: READING FROM THE QUR'AN — SURAT AN-NAS
## SUB-TOPIC: Reading Surat An-Nas in Arabic

### Reading Surat An-Nas
- Surat An-Nas has six verses.
- We read the transliteration of Surat An-Nas.
- Reading needs correct articulation.
<!-- source: lchk_1c39d6f24103439aa9a783db81b14e33 "transliteration" -->

### Verses of Surat An-Nas
- Qul a'oozu birabbin naas
- Malikin naas
- Ilaahin naas
- Min sharril waswaasil khannaas
- Allazee yuwaswisu fee sudoorin naas
- Minal jinnati wannaas
<!-- source: lchk_8e901e6e75984837a9b4f858f11ed702 "Qul a'oozu birabbin naas" -->

### Meaning of Surat An-Nas
- Verse one: I seek refuge with the Lord of mankind.
- Verse two: The King of mankind.
- Verse three: The God of mankind.
<!-- source: lchk_1c39d6f24103439aa9a783db81b14e33 "The King (or Ruler) Of Mankind" -->

### Note
- The six verses are read in order.
- Reading is practised in groups and individually.
