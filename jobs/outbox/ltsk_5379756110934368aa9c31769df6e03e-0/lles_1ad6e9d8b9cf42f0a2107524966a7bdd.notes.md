### Further details about human rights
- There was detention (imprisonment) without trial.
- There was massive killing of people during Amin’s regime
- Women were raped
- Political opponents were kidnapped and never to be seen again
- Amin declared an economic war which brought about scarcity of essential goods like soap, salt, sugar etc. Example of people who were killed during Amin’s regime
- Archibishop Janan Luwum
- Erinayo Oryema in 1977 Dumping grounds for bodies of some of the murdered people during Amin’s regime.
- Owen falls dam.
- Violation of human rights by some departments like police etc
