# TOPIC: LENGTH, MASS AND CAPACITY
## SUB-TOPIC: Converting square units

### New words
- square metre, square centimetre, square kilometre, area.

### Square units
- Area is measured in square units.
- $1\text{ m}=100\text{ cm}$.
- $1\text{ m}^2=100\text{ cm}\times100\text{ cm}$.
- $1\text{ m}^2=10000\text{ cm}^2$.
- $1\text{ km}^2=1000\text{ m}\times1000\text{ m}=1000000\text{ m}^2$.
<!-- source: lchk_03f8b377dc464cf8a3543c4f833ea50d "NOTE: area is measured in square units" -->

### Example: square metres to square centimetres
- Write $4\text{ m}^2$ as square centimetres.
- $1\text{ m}^2=10000\text{ cm}^2$.
- $4\text{ m}^2=4\times10000\text{ cm}^2$.
- $4\text{ m}^2=40000\text{ cm}^2$.
<!-- source: lchk_03f8b377dc464cf8a3543c4f833ea50d "Write 4m 2 as cm 2" -->

### Example: square kilometres to square metres
- Express $5\text{ km}^2$ as square metres.
- $1\text{ km}^2=1000000\text{ m}^2$.
- $5\text{ km}^2=5\times1000000\text{ m}^2$.
- $5\text{ km}^2=5000000\text{ m}^2$.
<!-- source: lchk_03f8b377dc464cf8a3543c4f833ea50d "Express 5km 2 as m 2" -->

### Example: square centimetres to square metres
- Change $190000\text{ cm}^2$ to square metres.
- $10000\text{ cm}^2=1\text{ m}^2$.
- $190000\text{ cm}^2=190000\div10000\text{ m}^2$.
- $190000\text{ cm}^2=19\text{ m}^2$.
