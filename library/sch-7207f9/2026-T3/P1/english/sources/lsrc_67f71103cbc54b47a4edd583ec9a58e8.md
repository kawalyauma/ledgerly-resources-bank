Using articles “a” and “an”
“A” is used when a noun begins with a consonant sound.
“An” is used when a noun begins with a vowel sound.
Using article “An” on words and in sentences.
“an” is used on single nouns that begin with vowel sounds e.g. a, e , I, o , u
Examples of single nouns.

an egg an umbrella
an owl an ant

an inkpot an ostrich, etc
“a” is used on single nouns that begin with consonant sounds e.g.
a book a snake
a chair a pencil
a table a door
