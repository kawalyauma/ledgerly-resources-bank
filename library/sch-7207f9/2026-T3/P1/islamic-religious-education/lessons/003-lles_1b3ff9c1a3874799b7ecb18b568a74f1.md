# TOPIC: READING FROM THE QUR'AN — SURAT AN-NAS
## SUB-TOPIC: Week 1 review

### Summary: Reciting Surat An-Nas
- Surat An-Nas has six verses.
- Recitation needs correct articulation.
- Verse 1: Qul a'oozu birabbin naas.
- Verse 2: Malikin naas.
- Verse 3: Ilaahin naas.

### Summary: Reading Surat An-Nas
- Verse 4: Min sharril waswaasil khannaas.
- Verse 5: Allazee yuwaswisu fee sudoorin naas.
- Verse 6: Minal jinnati wannaas.
- The verses can be read from a displayed chart.
- The surah is practised in groups and individually.

### Note
- Surat An-Nas is from the Holy Qur’an.
- I can read Surat An-Nas.

### Exercise
1. Recite the Surat.
2. Read Surat An-Nas.
