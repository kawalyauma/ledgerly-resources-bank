# TOPIC: READING FROM THE QUR'AN — SURAT AN-NAS
## SUB-TOPIC: Meaning of Surat An-Nas
### Meaning of Surat An-Nas
- Verse 1: I seek refuge with the Lord and Cherisher of Mankind.
- Verse 2: The King or Ruler of Mankind.
- Verse 3: The God or Judge of Mankind.
- Verse 4: From the mischief of the Whisperer of Evil who withdraws.
- Verse 5: The same who whispers into the hearts of Mankind.
- Verse 6: Among Jinns and among Men.
### Note
- Surat An-Nas has six verses.
