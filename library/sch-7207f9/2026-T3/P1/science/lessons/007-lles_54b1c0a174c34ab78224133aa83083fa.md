# TOPIC: TRANSPORT
## SUB-TOPIC: Road signs

### New words
- road, sign, stop, school, zebra, crossing, parking.

### Meaning of road signs
- Road signs are symbols put on roads to guide road users.

### Signs on the road
- Stop
- School ahead
- Zebra crossing
- Railway crossing
- No parking

### Importance of road signs
- Road signs help to reduce traffic jams.
- Road signs help to reduce road accidents.

### Zebra crossing
- A zebra crossing is a safe place for crossing a road.
