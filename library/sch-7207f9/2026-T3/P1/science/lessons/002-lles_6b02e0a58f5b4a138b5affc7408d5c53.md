# TOPIC: TRANSPORT
## SUB-TOPIC: Water transport

### New words
- water, boat, ship, canoe, ferry, captain, sailor.

### Meaning of water transport
- Water transport is the movement of people and goods on water.

### Means of water transport
- Boats
- Ferries
- Canoes
- Ships

### People who use water transport
- A captain operates a ship.
- A sailor sails a boat.

### Problems on water
- Bad weather
- Dangerous animals, such as crocodiles

### Exercise
1. Write the means of transport for water transport.
2. A person who sails a ship is a __________.
3. A person who sails a boat is a __________.
4. Draw these means of water transport: boat and ship.
