# TOPIC: TRANSPORT
## SUB-TOPIC: Railway transport

### New words
- railway, rails, train, station, goods.

### Meaning of railway transport
- Railway transport is the movement of people and goods on rails.
- The rails are built on land.

### Means of railway transport
- A train moves on rails.
- A train carries heavy things, such as food.

### Place where we find a train
- Trains are found at a railway station.
