# TOPIC: TRANSPORT
## SUB-TOPIC: Safe ways of using the road

### New words
- safe, road, zebra, crossing, footpath, pedestrian.

### Safe ways of using the road
- Use a zebra crossing.
- Obey traffic rules.
- Do not run while crossing the road.
- Follow traffic lights.
- Look right, left and right again before crossing.
- Use footpaths when walking along the road.

### Unsafe ways of using the road
- Playing on the road
- Speeding
- Driving while drunk

### Exercise
1. Underline the safe ways of using the road: follow the traffic lights; over-speeding; obey traffic rules.
2. Write down any two good ways of using the road.
