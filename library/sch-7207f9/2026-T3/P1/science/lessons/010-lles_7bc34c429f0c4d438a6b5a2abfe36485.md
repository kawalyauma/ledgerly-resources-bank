# TOPIC: TRANSPORT
## SUB-TOPIC: Week 2 review

### New words
- road, sign, traffic, lights, safe, footpath.

### Summary: road transport
- Road transport moves people and goods on land.
- Buses, cars, lorries and bicycles use roads.

### Summary: road signs
- Road signs guide road users.
- A zebra crossing is a safe place to cross a road.

### Summary: traffic lights
- Red means stop; orange means get ready; green means go.

### Summary: safe road use
- Obey traffic rules.
- Look right, left and right again before crossing.
- Use footpaths.
- Do not run while crossing a road.

### Exercise
1. Write four means of road transport.
2. Write any one importance of road signs to road users.
3. Name the three colours of the traffic lights.
4. Write the meaning of the traffic lights: red, orange and green.
5. Write down any two good ways of using the road.
