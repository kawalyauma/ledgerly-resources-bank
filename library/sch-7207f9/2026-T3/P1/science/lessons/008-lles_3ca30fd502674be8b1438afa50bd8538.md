# TOPIC: TRANSPORT
## SUB-TOPIC: Traffic lights

### New words
- traffic, lights, red, orange, green, stop.

### Three colours of the traffic lights
- Red
- Orange
- Green

### Meaning of the traffic lights
- Red means stop.
- Orange means get ready.
- Green means go.

### Exercise
1. Name the three colours of the traffic lights.
2. Write the meaning of the traffic lights: red, orange and green.
3. Draw and shade the traffic lights.
