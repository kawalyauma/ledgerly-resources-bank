# TOPIC: TRANSPORT
## SUB-TOPIC: Road transport

### New words
- road, tarmac, murram, bus, car, lorry, pedestrian.

### Meaning of road transport
- Road transport is the movement of people and goods on land.

### Types of roads
- Tarmac roads
- Murram roads

### Means of road transport
- Buses
- Cars
- Lorries
- Bicycles
- Motorcycles

### People who use road transport
- Drivers
- Passengers
- Pedestrians
- Cyclists
