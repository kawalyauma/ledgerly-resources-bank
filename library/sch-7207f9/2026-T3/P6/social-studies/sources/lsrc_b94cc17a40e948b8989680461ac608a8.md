TOWNSHIPS
These were separate homes for educated blacks who were working in large
cities in South Africa.
Examples of townships
 Soweto
 Sharpeville
 Nyanga.
 Kwa Thema
 Evaton
NB
Townships were characterized by poor housing conditions, shortage of social
services, poor wate management.
The fight/struggle to end apartheid.

 Frontline states.
 Organization of African Unity (OAU).
 Commonwealth Organization (CWO).
 Political parties.
