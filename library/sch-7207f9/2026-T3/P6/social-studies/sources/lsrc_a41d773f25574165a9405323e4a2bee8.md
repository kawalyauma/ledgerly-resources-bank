a) Home

NOTE: All Ugandans who died for the good of their country (Uganda) are
known as Heroes.

Why Heroes day is celebrated every year in Uganda
 To remember the people who died for the good of Uganda.
 To recognize Ugandans who have contributed to her social, economic
and political development.
