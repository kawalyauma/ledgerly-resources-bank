# TOPIC: FRIENDS ON THE WAY
## SUB-TOPIC: WEEK 1 REVIEW

### Summary: Meaning and importance of friendship

- Friendship is a relationship between people who care for each other.
- A true friend is someone with whom we relate freely.
- Friends share feelings and provide advice, encouragement and companionship.
- Friendship makes us feel loved, supported and more confident.
- True friends provide help in times of sickness and difficulty.

### Summary: Qualities of true friends

- Good friends are honest, patient, humble, forgiving and respectful.
- They show kindness, trust, tolerance and loyalty to one another.
- Friends give advice that encourages peace and responsible decisions.
- They support each other during a crisis with practical help.
- Jesus showed friendship by serving others and sacrificing for people.
