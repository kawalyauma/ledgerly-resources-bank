# TOPIC: FRIENDS ON THE WAY
## SUB-TOPIC: QUALITIES OF TRUE FRIENDS

### Qualities of true friends

- **Forgiveness** allows friends to restore their relationship after a disagreement.
- True friends are able to forgive when somebody disappoints them.
- **Patience** helps friends allow time for each other to improve.
- **Tolerance** helps friends accept other people's weaknesses.
- **Respect** requires friends to value one another's feelings and dignity.
- **Humility** helps friends serve others without considering themselves superior.
- True friends show kindness, honesty, openness and trustworthiness.
- Friends should be loyal and faithful when facing difficulties together.
- A selfless friend gives help without expecting immediate personal rewards.

### Advice given by friends

- **Advice** is an opinion or suggestion about what another person should do.
- Good advice helps friends to make wise and responsible decisions.
- Good advice encourages peace, friendship, respect and honest conduct.
- Good advice may protect people from danger and serious mistakes.
- Bad advice encourages dishonesty, cruelty, quarrels or harmful conduct.
- Friends should consider whether advice promotes care and responsible choices.

### Helping friends during a crisis

- **Crisis** means a dangerous or difficult situation needing serious attention.
- True friends encourage one another when difficulties cause fear or confusion.
- Friends may offer food, money, prayer, medicine, company or shelter.
- Friends should support people who have been falsely accused or treated unfairly.
- A loyal friend gives hope and encouragement during grief or hardship.
- Support may include advice, practical assistance and time spent together.

### Jesus' example of friendship

- Jesus taught his followers to love one another sincerely.
- He cared for people who were sick, hungry or in difficulty.
- Jesus washed his disciples' feet and served others with humility.
- He accepted suffering for others, showing genuine sacrificial love.
- Christians are encouraged to imitate his kindness and unselfishness.
