# TOPIC: CHRISTIAN INVOLVEMENT IN THE WORLD
## SUB-TOPIC: WAYS OF COOPERATION

### Meaning of cooperation
- Cooperation means working together to reach a desired goal.
- Christians cooperate when they work towards common objectives.
- Cooperation helps people overcome common problems.
- Working together encourages people to support each other.

### Ways of cooperation
- By working together as a team.
- By practising good and open leadership.
- By supporting democratic laws and rules.
- By showing concern for the welfare of others.
- By working hard towards a shared goal.
- By providing peace, freedom and security.
- By caring properly for God's creation.
- By allowing people to share useful ideas.

### Examples of Christian voluntary organisations
- The Young Women's Christian Association serves people.
- The Bible Society spreads the word of God.
- World Vision International builds and facilitates schools.
- The Mother's Union helps people in communities.
- The Christian Children's Fund supports needy children.
- The Adventist Development Relief Agency helps communities.
- Scripture Union promotes teaching the word of God.

### Ways Christians care for others
- By providing food to people who are hungry.
- By helping those who are weak.
- By being generous to other people.
- By caring for people who are sick.
- By offering support to people in sorrow.
- By acting fairly towards other people.
- By sharing needed resources with others.
