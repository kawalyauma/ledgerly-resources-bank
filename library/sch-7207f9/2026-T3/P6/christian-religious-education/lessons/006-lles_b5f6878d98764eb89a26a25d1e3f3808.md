# TOPIC: FRIENDS ON THE WAY
## SUB-TOPIC: WEEK 2 REVIEW

### Summary: Good and bad advice

- Advice means an opinion or suggestion about what someone should do.
- Good advice encourages wise choices, honesty and respect.
- Good advice can protect people from unnecessary danger.
- Bad advice encourages cheating, jealousy, conflict or disobedience.
- Careful people examine advice before choosing to follow it.

### Summary: Marriage in Christian life

- Marriage is a recognised family union between spouses.
- The lesson identifies religious, civil and customary forms of marriage.
- Good married partners show kindness, patience and trust.
- Faithfulness, respect and forgiveness help family relationships.
- Marriage vows are promises showing a couple's commitment to each other.
