# TOPIC: FRIENDS ON THE WAY
## SUB-TOPIC: DISTINGUISHING GOOD AND BAD ADVICE

### Meaning of advice

- **Advice** means an opinion or suggestion on what a person should do.
- People seek advice when they must make important decisions in life.
- Friends may help one another make careful and responsible decisions.
- Not all advice is good, even when offered by someone familiar.
- Christians should examine advice before deciding whether to follow it.
- Poor advice may misguide a person into making wrong decisions.

### Qualities of good advice

- Good advice encourages people to do things that please God.
- Good advice helps friends to make careful and wise decisions.
- Good advice promotes peace rather than hatred or misunderstanding.
- Good advice promotes respect for other people's dignity and feelings.
- Good advice encourages friends to work hard and serve others.
- Good advice encourages honesty, trustworthiness and faithfulness.
- Good advice encourages obedience and responsible conduct in society.
- Good advice encourages unity between friends and community members.

### Bad advice

- Bad advice encourages people to use abusive words against others.
- Bad advice may promote hatred and jealousy between friends.
- Bad advice may encourage people to cheat or tell lies.
- Bad advice may encourage disobedience and unfaithfulness.
- Bad advice may make people biased when judging one another.
- Bad advice may encourage people to seek revenge after disputes.
- Bad advice may lead to quarrels, disunity, suffering or misery.

### Importance of good advice

- Useful advice helps us make good decisions and avoid danger.
- Useful advice reduces worry and the chance of making mistakes.
- Good advice strengthens friendship by encouraging peaceful choices.
- Useful advice can promote responsibility and honest cooperation.
- Good advice can bring happiness and unity to a community.
- Careful advice can protect a person's plans from avoidable failure.
