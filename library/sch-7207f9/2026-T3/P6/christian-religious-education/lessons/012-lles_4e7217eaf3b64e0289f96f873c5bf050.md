# TOPIC: CHRISTIAN INVOLVEMENT IN THE WORLD
## SUB-TOPIC: Week 4 review
### Summary: Christian voluntary organisations
- Voluntary organisations are groups of people who work willingly and without payment.
- Voluntary organisations do charity work.
- Voluntary organisations offer free services.
- Young Women’s Christian Association (YWCA)
- The Bible Society
- World Vision International
- Mother’s Union
- Christian Children’s Fund (C.C.F)
- Adventist Development Relief Agency (A.D.R.A)
- Inter Religious Council (IRC)
- Uganda Joint Christian Council (U.J.C.C)
- Fathers’ Union
- Scripture Union
### Summary: Role of Christian organisations
- Christian organisations offer free services to people and communities.
- Some promote the welfare of orphans and help fight poverty.
- Some promote good health and provide safe water sources.
- Christian Children’s Fund sponsors needy children in education and provides health care.
- World Vision International supports orphans, builds schools and clinics, and provides safe water.
- ADRA builds and facilitates schools and caters for people hit by disasters.
- Compassion International caters for orphans and needy children.
- YWCA provides education, sponsors students and provides counselling.
- Mother’s Union promotes women’s projects and offers counselling.
- Catholic Women’s Guild promotes women’s projects.
- Scripture Union promotes the teaching of God’s word in schools and institutions.
- Christian organisations promote health and care for disadvantaged people.
