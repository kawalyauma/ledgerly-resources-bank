# TOPIC: CHRISTIAN INVOLVEMENT IN THE WORLD
## SUB-TOPIC: Week 5 review

### Summary: Technology in Christian development
- **Science** is the study of the natural world based on facts learned through experiments and observation.
- **Technology** is the practical way of using science to solve day-to-day problems.
- Better roads improve transport.
- Speedy motorboats and aeroplanes cover long distances faster.
- Emails, telephones, radios and television improve communication.
- Modern technology helps construct better buildings and houses.
- Better farming methods and tools increase food production.
- Easy publication, typing and printing of materials improve education.
- Technology improves power production and entertainment.
- Machines and vehicle exhausts cause air and water pollution.
- Machines working at high speed increase unemployment.
- Movies bring different cultures into people’s lives.
- Accidents, fires, electric accidents and weapons increase death rates.

### Summary: Applications of technology
- Technology is applied in transport and communication.
- Technology helps construct better buildings and houses.
- Better farming methods, tools and fertilisers increase food production.
- Publication, typing and printing of materials improve education.
- Technology improves food preservation.
- Technology improves power production and entertainment.
