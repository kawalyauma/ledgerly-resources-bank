The Christian view about heaven
Christians believe that heaven is a place where they will meet face to face with their creator
They will live him in total fellowship and joy forever
They also believe that heaven is a place where all believers will meet in a gathering called a wedding feast of the lamb, there will be unspeakable joy and happiness (Rev 21:2-7)
Christians believe that heaven is a place where all believers will be rewarded for all their good deeds
Heaven itself will be rewards for righteous people while sinners go to hell.
Heaven is a place of eternal rest for believers.

TITLE: PURITY
Purity refers to the state of being pure, clean and not being mixed with anything unclean.
Christian purity
Purity is one requirement Christian need to enter God’s kingdom / to go to heaven
Jesus tells us to be prepared to all times to enter into God’s kingdom. He says that nobody knows the hour of death so we should always be prepared (Mt 25:1-13)
Ways through which we can purify ourselves
As human beings we are not perfect although we are called to be.
God wants us to continually purify ourselves, sins prevents us from going to heaven
Some of the ways we can purify ourselves in order to go to heaven include:-
Repenting our sins
Forgiving others
Praying to God daily
Having courage and perseverance as we seek God
Reading the bible
Attending fellowship at church
Reading Christian literature
Showing love to others in what we do and say.
