# TOPIC: ACCIDENTS AND FIRST AID
## SUB-TOPIC: Accidents and first aid

### Accidents
- An **accident** is a sudden happening that causes harm to the body.
- Accidents can take place anywhere.
- Accidents may happen at home.
- Accidents may happen at school.
- Accidents may happen at a place of work.
- Accidents may happen where people go for prayers.
- Accidents may happen on the pitch.

### Injuries to the body
- Fainting
- Animal bites
- Nose bleeding
- Bruises
- Choking
- Food poisoning
- Burns
- Scalds
- Cuts
- Foreign bodies in natural openings

### First aid and casualty
- **First aid** is the immediate help given to a casualty before being taken to the nearest health centre.
- A **casualty** is a person involved in an accident who needs first aid assistance.
- A **first aider** is a person who gives first help to a casualty.
- A first aider may give assistance to a casualty.

### Reasons for giving first aid
- First aid saves life.
- First aid promotes quick recovery.
- First aid stops bleeding.
- First aid reduces pain.
- First aid prevents further injuries.

### First aid kit
- A **first aid kit** is equipment used when giving first aid for a certain type of accident.
- Cotton wool
- Plaster
- Razor blade
- Bandage
- Pain killers
- A pair of scissors

### Qualities of a good first aider
- A good first aider should be quick in giving first aid.
- A good first aider should be knowledgeable.
- A good first aider should have skills.
- A good first aider should be clean.

### Note
- First aid is given before a casualty is taken to a health centre.
- First aid can save life and prevent further injuries.
- A first aider should be quick, knowledgeable, skilled and clean.
