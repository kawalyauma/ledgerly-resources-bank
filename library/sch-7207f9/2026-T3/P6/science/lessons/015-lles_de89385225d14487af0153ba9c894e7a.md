# TOPIC: ACCIDENTS AND FIRST AID
## SUB-TOPIC: Causes of fever

### Fever
- **Fever** is a condition of the body when temperature goes beyond normal.
- Normal body temperature is 37°C or 98.4°F.
- Fever is not a disease.
- Fever is a symptom of many diseases.
- Fever may be a symptom of malaria, measles, meningitis or typhoid.
- Fever can lead to convulsions.
- High fever causes convulsions.

### Causes of fever and convulsions
- Diseases or illness like malaria can cause fever and convulsions.
- Measles can cause fever and convulsions.
- Meningitis can cause fever and convulsions.
- Typhoid can cause fever and convulsions.
- Epilepsy, also called fits, is listed as a cause.
- Exposure of the body to high temperature is a cause.
- Poisoning is a cause.
- High fever causes convulsions.

### Note
- Fever is a symptom of many diseases, not a disease itself.
- Malaria, measles, meningitis and typhoid are listed illnesses.
- Exposure to high temperature and poisoning are listed causes.
