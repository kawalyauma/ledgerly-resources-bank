# TOPIC: ACCIDENTS AND FIRST AID
## SUB-TOPIC: Fever and convulsions

### Fever
- **Fever** is a condition when body temperature goes beyond normal.
- Normal body temperature is 37°C or 98.4°F.
- Fever is not a disease.
- Fever is a symptom of many diseases.
- Fever may be a symptom of malaria, measles, meningitis or typhoid.
- Fever can lead to convulsions.
- High fever causes convulsions.

### Convulsions
- **Convulsions** are sudden violent body movements that cannot be controlled.
- Convulsions are uncontrollable jerky movements of the body.
- Convulsions are involuntary shaking or jerking of the body.
- Epilepsy is also called fits.
- Convulsions can be stopped if the disease causing them is treated.

### First aid for convulsions
- Make the person get enough air supply.
- Remove all tight clothes and loosen other clothes.
- Clear the space where the victim is convulsing.
- Put an object between the teeth to prevent tongue biting.
- Give the victim plenty of cold drinks after recovery.
- Take the patient to hospital.

### Note
- Fever is a symptom of disease, not a disease itself.
- High fever can cause convulsions.
- Convulsions are uncontrolled body movements.
