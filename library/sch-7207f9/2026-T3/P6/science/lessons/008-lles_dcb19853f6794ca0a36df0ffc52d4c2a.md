# TOPIC: SCIENCE AT HOME AND IN OUR COMMUNITY
## SUB-TOPIC: Sorting and soaking clothes

### Cleaning clothes at home
- One main use of water at home is to wash clothes.
- Washing clothes at home is also called laundry.
- Cleaning clothes at home has several steps.
- Sorting comes before soaking, washing and rinsing.

### Sorting
- **Sorting** involves selecting and putting clothes together.
- Clothes are sorted according to colour.
- Clothes are sorted according to intensity of dirt.
- Clothes are sorted according to the nature of the fabric.
- Clothes are sorted according to the use of clothes.
- Sorting separates clothes according to their features.
- Sorting is the first stated step in cleaning clothes at home.

### Soaking
- **Soaking** is putting clothes in soapy water for some time.
- Soaking helps to loosen dirt.
- Soaking helps to dissolve stains.
- Soaking saves time during washing.
- Soaking makes it take little effort to remove dirt.
- Soaking saves clothes against tear and wear.
- Constant rubbing during washing can cause tear and wear.
- Soaking is followed by washing.

### Order of the steps
- Sorting
- Soaking
- Washing
- Rinsing

### Note
- Sorting uses colour, intensity of dirt, nature of fabric and use.
- Soaking means putting clothes in soapy water for some time.
- Soaking loosens dirt and dissolves stains.
- Soaking saves time and reduces rubbing during washing.
