# TOPIC: SCIENCE AT HOME AND IN OUR COMMUNITY
## SUB-TOPIC: Washing and rinsing clothes

### Washing
- **Washing** is the removal of dirt using water and detergent.
- Washing follows soaking in cleaning clothes at home.
- Hand washing is one type of washing clothes.
- Machine washing is another type of washing clothes.
- Water and detergent are used to remove dirt.
- Washing removes dirt from clothes.

### Rinsing
- **Rinsing** is putting clothes in clean water.
- Rinsing helps to remove all soapy water from clothes.
- Clean water is used after washing clothes.
- Rinsing follows washing in the laundry steps.

### Wringing
- **Wringing** involves squeezing excess water out of clothes.
- Woollen clothes should be dried without wringing.
- Wringing may loosen woollen fabric.
- Wringing may make woollen clothes lose their shape.

### Drying and ironing
- Some clothes can be dried out completely under the sun.
- Ironing removes creases from washed clothes.
- Ironing helps clothes return to their original form.
- Ironing kills parasites like lice and itch mites.
- Ironing kills germs.

### Items used to clean clothes
- Clean water
- Detergents
- Soap
- Basin

### Order of laundry steps
- Sorting
- Soaking
- Washing
- Rinsing
- Wringing
- Drying
- Ironing

### Note
- Washing removes dirt using water and detergent.
- Rinsing removes soapy water from clothes.
- Woollen clothes should be dried without wringing.
- Ironing removes creases and kills parasites and germs.
