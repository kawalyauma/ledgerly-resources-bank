# TOPIC: ACCIDENTS AND FIRST AID
## SUB-TOPIC: Preventing burns

### Burns and their causes
- **Burns** are injuries caused by dry heat.
- A burn is an injury on the skin caused by dry heat.
- Burns can be caused by hot metals.
- Burns can be caused by hot flat irons.
- Burns can be caused by acids.
- Burns can be caused by glowing charcoal.
- Burns can be caused by hot charcoal stoves.
- Burns can be caused by fire.
- Burns can be caused by hot plates and cookers.
- Burns can be caused by un-insulated electric wires carrying current.

### Preventing burns
- Accidents may happen at home, school, work, places for prayers and on the pitch.
- Hot plates and cookers are listed causes of burns.
- Hot flat irons are listed causes of burns.
- Hot charcoal stoves, glowing charcoal and fire are listed causes of burns.
- Un-insulated electric wires carrying current are listed causes of burns.
- Acids and chemicals are listed causes of burns.
- Avoiding contact with the listed causes prevents contact burns.

### First aid facts linked to burn prevention
- Cool water reduces the temperature of a burnt part.
- First degree burns need no dressing.
- Breaking a blister may lead to infection of the wound by germs.
- Fats, oil, coffee, herbs or dung should never be put on a burn.
- These substances can cause infection.

### Note
- Burns are caused by heat and other listed contact causes.
- Avoid contact with hot objects, fire, acids and un-insulated wires.
- Breaking a blister may lead to wound infection.
