# TOPIC: ACCIDENTS AND FIRST AID
## SUB-TOPIC: First aid for fever

### First aid for fever
- Remove most of the person’s clothes.
- Carry out tepid sponging.
- Encourage the person to drink more cold fluids than normal.
- Tepid sponging is also called a cold compress.
- Put a wet cloth on the victim’s forehead or chest.
- Do not use very cold water.
- Very cold water can lead to convulsions.
- Fever is a condition when body temperature goes beyond normal.
- Normal body temperature is 37°C or 98.4°F.
- Fever is not an illness but a symptom of many illnesses.
- Malaria, typhoid and measles are examples of illnesses with fever.

### Note
- Tepid sponging uses a wet cloth on the forehead or chest.
- Do not use very cold water for tepid sponging.
- Encourage a person with fever to drink more cold fluids than normal.
