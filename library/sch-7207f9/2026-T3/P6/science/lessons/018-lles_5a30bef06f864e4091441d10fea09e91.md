# TOPIC: ACCIDENTS AND FIRST AID
## SUB-TOPIC: First aid for near drowning

### First aid for near drowning
- Yell for help.
- Remove the person from water as soon as possible.
- Remove the victim quickly from water.
- Shout for help.
- Lie the person on his back.
- Carry out mouth-to-mouth breathing, also called kiss of life.
- A nearly drowned person has only four minutes to live.

### Mouth-to-mouth breathing
- Make the victim lie on his back.
- Tilt the head backwards and keep the mouth open.
- Remove any object stuck in the mouth.
- Press the victim’s nostrils with fingers to close them.
- Put your mouth directly into the victim’s mouth.
- Blow so that the chest rises.
- Stop briefly to let the air out and blow again.
- Repeat many times, about 15 times in a minute.
- Continue until the victim can breathe again by himself.
- Place the heels of your hands between the navel and ribs.
- Make a quick strong push forward into the ribcage.

### Note
- Remove the victim quickly from water.
- Shout for help.
- A nearly drowned person has only four minutes to live.
- Continue mouth-to-mouth breathing until the victim breathes again by himself.
