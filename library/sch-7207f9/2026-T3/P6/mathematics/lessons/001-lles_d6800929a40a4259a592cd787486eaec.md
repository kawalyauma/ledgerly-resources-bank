# TOPIC: LENGTH, MASS AND CAPACITY
## SUB-TOPIC: Metric units of length

### New words
- length, kilometre, metre, conversion.

### Metric length units
- Length may be recorded in centimetres and millimetres.
- $1\text{ km}=1000\text{ m}$.
- $1\text{ m}=100\text{ cm}$.
- $1\text{ m}=1000\text{ mm}$.

### Example: kilometres to metres
- Change $7\text{ km}$ to metres.
- $1\text{ km}=1000\text{ m}$.
- $7\text{ km}=7\times1000\text{ m}$.
- $7\text{ km}=7000\text{ m}$.
- Find the number of metres in $4.7\text{ km}$.
- $4.7\text{ km}=4.7\times1000\text{ m}=4700\text{ m}$.

### Example: metres to kilometres
- Write $42000\text{ m}$ as kilometres.
- $1000\text{ m}=1\text{ km}$.
- $42000\text{ m}=42000\div1000\text{ km}$.
- $42000\text{ m}=42\text{ km}$.
- Change $2500\text{ m}$ to kilometres.
- $2500\text{ m}=2500\div1000\text{ km}=2.5\text{ km}$.

### Exercise
1. Express 25 km in metres.
2. Convert 4 km to metres.
3. Change 800 m to km.
4. Change 7 km to metres.
