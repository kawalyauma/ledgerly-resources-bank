# TOPIC: LENGTH, MASS AND CAPACITY
## SUB-TOPIC: Area and perimeter of a square

### New words
- square, side, area, perimeter.

### Area and perimeter of squares
- A square has all its sides and angles equal.
- Perimeter is the total distance around a figure.
- $\text{Area}=\text{side}\times\text{side}$.
- $\text{Perimeter}=\text{side}+\text{side}+\text{side}+\text{side}$.

### Example: area from a side
- Find the area of a square whose side is $6\text{ cm}$.
- $\text{Area}=\text{side}\times\text{side}$.
- $\text{Area}=6\text{ cm}\times6\text{ cm}$.
- $\text{Area}=36\text{ cm}^2$.

### Example: side from area
- The area of a square is $64\text{ cm}^2$. Find each side.
- $p\times p=64$.
- $p^2=64$.
- $p=2\times2\times2$.
- Each side is $8\text{ cm}$.

### Example: area from perimeter
- The perimeter of a square is $24\text{ cm}$. What is its area?
- $\text{Side}=\text{perimeter}\div4$.
- $\text{Side}=24\div4=6\text{ cm}$.
- $\text{Area}=6\text{ cm}\times6\text{ cm}=36\text{ cm}^2$.
