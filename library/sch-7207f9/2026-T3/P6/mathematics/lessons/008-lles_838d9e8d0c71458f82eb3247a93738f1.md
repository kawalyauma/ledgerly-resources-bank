# TOPIC: LENGTH, MASS AND CAPACITY
## SUB-TOPIC: Area of rectangular borders

### New words
- border, outer length, outer width, inner area.

### Rectangular borders
- $\text{Shaded area}=\text{outer area}-\text{inner area}$.
- Area is measured in square units.

### Example: a two-centimetre border
- Inner length is $8\text{ cm}$ and inner width is $5\text{ cm}$.
- The border is $2\text{ cm}$ wide on every side.
- $\text{Outer length}=8+2+2=12\text{ cm}$.
- $\text{Outer width}=5+2+2=9\text{ cm}$.
- $\text{Outer area}=12\times9=108\text{ cm}^2$.
- $\text{Inner area}=8\times5=40\text{ cm}^2$.
- $\text{Border area}=108-40=68\text{ cm}^2$.

### Example: a three-centimetre border
- Inner length is $5\text{ cm}$ and inner width is $4\text{ cm}$.
- The border is $3\text{ cm}$ wide on every side.
- $\text{Outer length}=5+3+3=11\text{ cm}$.
- $\text{Outer width}=4+3+3=10\text{ cm}$.
- $\text{Outer area}=11\times10=110\text{ cm}^2$.
- $\text{Inner area}=5\times4=20\text{ cm}^2$.
- $\text{Border area}=110-20=90\text{ cm}^2$.

### Exercise
1. An inner rectangle is 8 cm long and 5 cm wide, with a 2 cm border on each side. Find the outer length.
2. For the same 2 cm border, find the outer width.
3. For the same 2 cm border, find the shaded area.
4. An inner rectangle is 5 cm by 4 cm, with a 3 cm border on each side. Find the outer length and width.
5. For the same 3 cm border, calculate the area outside the inner rectangle.
