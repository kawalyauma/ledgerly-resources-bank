# TOPIC: LENGTH, MASS AND CAPACITY
## SUB-TOPIC: Area and perimeter of a rectangle

### New words
- rectangle, length, width, area, perimeter.

### Area and perimeter of rectangles
- $\text{Area}=\text{length}\times\text{width}$.
- $\text{Width}=\text{area}\div\text{length}$.
- $\text{Length}=\text{area}\div\text{width}$.
- $\text{Perimeter}=2(\text{length}+\text{width})$.

### Example: width from area
- The area of a rectangle is $56\text{ cm}^2$ and its length is $8\text{ cm}$. Find its width.
- $\text{Length}\times\text{width}=\text{area}$.
- $8w=56$.
- $w=56\div8=7\text{ cm}$.

### Example: length from area
- A rectangular piece of paper has area $4800\text{ mm}^2$ and width $60\text{ mm}$. Find its length.
- $\text{Length}\times\text{width}=\text{area}$.
- $60L=4800$.
- $L=4800\div60=80\text{ mm}$.

### Example: perimeter from area
- A rectangle has area $28\text{ cm}^2$ and width $4\text{ cm}$. Find its perimeter.
- $\text{Length}=28\div4=7\text{ cm}$.
- $P=2(L+W)$.
- $P=2(7+4)=2\times11=22\text{ cm}$.
