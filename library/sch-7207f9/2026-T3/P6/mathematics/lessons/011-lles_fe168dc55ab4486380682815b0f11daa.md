# TOPIC: LENGTH, MASS AND CAPACITY
## SUB-TOPIC: Comparing areas: finding a height

### New words
- height, perpendicular, equal area, unknown.

### Finding a height
- $\text{Area}=\frac12\times\text{base}\times\text{height}$.
- $\text{Height}=2\times\text{area}\div\text{base}$.
- Two expressions for the same triangle’s area are equal.

### Example: height from two expressions for area
- In triangle UVW, $UV=12\text{ cm}$, $WY=8\text{ cm}$ and $WV=24\text{ cm}$.
- WY is perpendicular to UV; UX is perpendicular to WV.
- $\frac12\times24\times UX=\frac12\times12\times8$.
- $12UX=48$.
- $UX=48\div12=4\text{ cm}$.

### Example: another pair of heights
- In triangle ABC, $BC=100\text{ cm}$, $AC=86\text{ cm}$, $BD=10\text{ cm}$ and $AE=y$.
- BD is perpendicular to AC; AE is perpendicular to BC.
- $\frac12\times100\times y=\frac12\times86\times10$.
- $50y=430$.
- $y=430\div50=8.6\text{ cm}$.

### Example: height from area
- A triangle has area $144\text{ cm}^2$ and base $24\text{ cm}$.
- $\frac12\times24\times h=144$.
- $12h=144$.
- $h=144\div12=12\text{ cm}$.

### Exercise
1. In triangle UVW, UV = 12 cm, perpendicular height WY = 8 cm, and WV = 24 cm. Find perpendicular height UX to WV.
2. In triangle ABC, BC = 100 cm, AC = 86 cm and BD = 10 cm. BD is perpendicular to AC; AE is perpendicular to BC. Find AE.
3. Find the height of a triangle with area 144 square centimetres and base 24 cm.
4. A triangle has area 28 square centimetres and base 14 cm. Find its height h.
5. Find the height of a triangle with area 30 square centimetres and base 12 cm.
