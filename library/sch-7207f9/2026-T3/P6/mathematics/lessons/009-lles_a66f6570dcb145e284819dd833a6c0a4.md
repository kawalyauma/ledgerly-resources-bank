# TOPIC: LENGTH, MASS AND CAPACITY
## SUB-TOPIC: Area of triangles

### New words
- triangle, base, height, perpendicular, area.

### Area of a triangle
- The height is perpendicular to the base.
- $\text{Area}=\frac12\times\text{base}\times\text{height}$.
- A triangle can be completed to form a rectangle or parallelogram.
- Its area is half the completed rectangle or parallelogram.

### Example: base four centimetres
- A triangle has base $4\text{ cm}$ and height $3\text{ cm}$.
- $A=\frac12\times4\text{ cm}\times3\text{ cm}$.
- $A=2\times3=6\text{ cm}^2$.

### Example: base ten centimetres
- A triangle has base $10\text{ cm}$ and height $6\text{ cm}$.
- $A=\frac12\times10\text{ cm}\times6\text{ cm}$.
- $A=5\times6=30\text{ cm}^2$.

### Example: base in two parts
- The base consists of $4\text{ cm}$ and $2\text{ cm}$.
- $\text{Base}=4+2=6\text{ cm}$.
- The perpendicular height is $8\text{ cm}$.
- $A=\frac12\times6\text{ cm}\times8\text{ cm}$.
- $A=3\times8=24\text{ cm}^2$.

### Exercise
1. Find the area of a triangle with base 25 cm and height 30 cm.
2. Find the area of a triangle with base 16 cm and height 4 cm.
3. Find the area of a triangle with base 12 cm and height 8 cm.
4. Two adjacent triangles have common height 6 cm and bases 4 cm and 5 cm. Find the area of the triangle with base 4 cm.
5. For the same adjacent triangles, calculate the area of the whole figure.
