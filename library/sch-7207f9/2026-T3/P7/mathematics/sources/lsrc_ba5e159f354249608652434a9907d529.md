Divisibility for 2.
A Number is divisible by 2 if the digit in the one‟s place is 0, 2, 4, 6, or 8 eg 1460
Test for 3:
A number is divisible by 3 if the sum of its digits is divisible by 3 eg 741 = 7 + 4 + 1 = 12
Test for 4:
A number is divisible by 4 if the number formed by its last two digits is divisible by 4. eg
572. The last two digits are 7 and 2 therefore the number formed is 72 which is divisible
by 4. Hence 572 is divisible by 4.
Test for five (5):
A number is divisible by 5 if the last digit in the ones place is either 0 or 5. eg 360 or
805.
