# TOPIC: STRENGTHENED BY THE SPIRIT WE ACCEPT THE EVENTS OF LIFE
## SUB-TOPIC: Fear in people and animals

### Meaning of fear
- **Fear** is the feeling that something bad may happen.

### Forms of fear
- Real fear is being afraid of objects that can harm.
- Imagined fear is general fear of failure.
- Anxiety is a state of being worried.

### Causes of fear in people
- Being lonely.
- Lack of confidence.
- Death.
- Embarrassment.

### Causes of fear in animals
- The curriculum includes causes of fear in animals.
- The passages do not name those causes.

### Value of fear
- Fear helps us avoid dangerous situations.
- Fear makes us do tasks better.
- Fear makes us respect those who can protect us.
- Fear makes us seek God’s help.

### Bible teaching on fear and courage
- Mark 14:32–36 teaches prayer to God for strength and courage.
- Joshua 1:5–9 teaches determination, confidence and obedience to God.
- Isaiah 41:10–14 teaches leaving worries to God.

### Note
- Fear can help people avoid danger.
- The curriculum asks learners to discuss causes of fear in animals.
