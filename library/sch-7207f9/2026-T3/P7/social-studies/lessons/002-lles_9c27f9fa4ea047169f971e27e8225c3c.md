# TOPIC: ECONOMIC DEVELOPMENTS IN AFRICA
## SUB-TOPIC: Land and soil resources
### Importance of land and soils
- Land and soils are major economic resources of Africa.
- Mountain slopes and lake shores have fertile soils.
- Fertile soils support crop cultivation.
- Fertile soil is a factor leading to high population.
- Mountains provide building materials, such as stones.
- Hills and mountains provide sites for telecommunication masts.
- Physical features form natural boundaries between places and districts.
### Reasons for introducing cash crops in Uganda
- To enable Africans to get money to pay taxes.
- To develop African countries economically.
- To get raw materials for their home industries.
- To utilise the free fertile land in Africa.
### Problems faced in mountainous areas
- Mountains hinder agricultural mechanisation.
- Physical features hinder road construction.
- Some mountains erupt and destroy property.
- Steep slopes promote soil erosion.
- Mountains are greatly affected by landslides.
### Ways of caring for land
- Planting trees on mountain slopes.
- Terracing land controls soil erosion in highlands.
- Filling holes after mining.
