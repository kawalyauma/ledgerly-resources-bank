# TOPIC: ECONOMIC DEVELOPMENTS IN AFRICA
## SUB-TOPIC: Major economic resources of Africa
### New words
resource, natural resources
### Major economic resources of Africa
- Land and soils
- Minerals
- Animals
- People
- Vegetation
- Climate
- Water
### Importance of natural resources
- Lakes and rivers are a source of fish.
- Lakes and rivers help in the formation of rain.
- Lakes and rivers provide water for domestic use.
- Rivers help in the generation of hydroelectricity.
- Lakes and rivers provide water for irrigation.
- Physical features provide income through tourism.
- Mountain slopes and lake shores have fertile soils.
- Fertile soils support crop cultivation.
- Mountains provide building materials, such as stones.
- Osukuru hills in Tororo provide limestone.
### Problems faced in utilising resources
- Lakes and rivers flood, killing people.
- Mountains hinder agricultural mechanisation.
- Some mountains erupt and destroy property.
- Steep slopes promote soil erosion.
- Mountains are greatly affected by landslides.
### Ways of caring for resources
- Planting trees on mountain slopes and river banks.
- Terracing land controls soil erosion in highlands.
- Using proper fishing methods.
- Filling holes after mining.
- Treating wastes before disposal in water bodies.
