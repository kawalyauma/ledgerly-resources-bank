31. What is Inductive method of teaching ?
14
Inductive method of teaching is where a teacher uses examples, and from those examples
given, pupils discover the rules, principles or concept being used e.g. a teacher may write
the following series in the chalkboard and let the pupils discover the rule (s) used.
30, 26, 29, 25, 28, 27, 23
Some pupils may come up with their discovery that subtraction and addition are being
used, thus minus 4 add 3.
Advantages
1) Pupils are trained to think and work out problems for themselves
2) Retention of facts is enhanced
3) It develops logical thinking and creativity
4) It encourages investigation
5) It trains pupils to tackle real problems.
Disadvantages:
1) It is time consuming
2) Pupils with low I.Q may not see the connection between the example given and the
rule or principle formed.
3) Unless the teacher is skilled in questioning the objective may not be achieved.
