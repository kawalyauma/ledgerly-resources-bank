# TOPIC: ALGEBRA
## SUB-TOPIC: Using letters for numbers — Equations with and without letters
### Using letters for numbers
- A letter can stand for a number of objects.
- Let each pen be $p$.
- One pen is $1p$.
- One pen and one pen make $2p$.
- One pen, one pen and one pen make $3p$.
- One pen, one pen, one pen and one pen make $4p$.
- Let $m$ stand for mangoes.
- Three mangoes are $3m$.
- One mango is $1m$.
- Seven mangoes are $7m$.
### Examples
- Two balls and two balls make four balls.
- Two bananas and two bananas make four bananas.
- Four cows and ten cows make fourteen cows.
- Two dogs and five dogs make seven dogs.
- Nine cats and five cats make fourteen cats.
- Five eggs and five eggs make ten eggs.
### Note
- A letter stands for the number of objects named.
- $1p+1p+1p+1p=4p$.
- $3m+1m+7m=11m$.
### Exercise
1. Let each pen be $p$. Write one pen and one pen in short.
2. Write two balls and two balls as a total number of balls.
3. How many bananas are two bananas and two bananas?
4. How many cows are four cows and ten cows?
