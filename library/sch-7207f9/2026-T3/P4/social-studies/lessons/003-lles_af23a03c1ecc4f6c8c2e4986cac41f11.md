# TOPIC: OUR LEADERS IN THE DISTRICT
## SUB-TOPIC: BY-LAWS

### Meaning of by-laws
- By-laws are laws made by local councils.
- Local councils make laws for their local communities.
- Local councillors help to maintain village order.
- Local leaders implement government policies.

### Importance of by-laws
- By-laws help settle disputes among people.
- They help promote order in society.
- They are used to promote peace and security.
- They promote respect for people's rights.

### Duties of local councils
- Local councils settle disputes among neighbours.
- Local councils maintain law and order.
- They implement government policies such as immunisation.
- They promote sanitation within the village.
- They protect community members and their property.
- They unite different people within villages.
- They give information on government programmes.
