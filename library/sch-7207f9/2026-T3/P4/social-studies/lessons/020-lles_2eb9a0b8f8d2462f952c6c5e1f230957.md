# TOPIC: OUR LEADERS IN THE DISTRICT
## SUB-TOPIC: RIGHTS OF DISTRICT RESIDENTS — PART B

### Remember
- Residents must respect other people's rights.
- People should report human rights abuse.
- Children have a right to education.
- Children have a right to medical treatment.
- Children have a right to protection.
- Citizens should obey the laws of Uganda.
- Citizens should pay taxes promptly.
- People should help maintain law and order.
- Residents should care for sick people.
- Citizens should participate in community work.
- People should help maintain proper sanitation.
- Residents should participate in productive activities.
- Citizenship means legal belonging to a country.
