# TOPIC: OUR LEADERS IN THE DISTRICT
## SUB-TOPIC: TYPES OF DISTRICT LEADERS

### Types of leaders in the district
- Uganda has political leaders.
- Uganda has civic leaders.
- Some leaders are voluntary leaders.
- Religious leaders serve worshipping communities.
- Cultural leaders serve traditional communities.
- Local political leaders are elected.
- Civic leaders are appointed.

### Examples of political leaders
- A president is a political leader.
- Members of Parliament are political leaders.
- Mayors are political leaders.
- Local council chairpersons are political leaders.
- Councillors are political leaders.
- Political leaders are chosen through voting.

### Civic leaders
- Civic leaders provide social services.
- Civic leaders obtain positions by appointment.
- The CAO is a civic leader.
- The DEO manages district educational programmes.
- The DDHS supervises health centres.
- The district water officer distributes clean water.
