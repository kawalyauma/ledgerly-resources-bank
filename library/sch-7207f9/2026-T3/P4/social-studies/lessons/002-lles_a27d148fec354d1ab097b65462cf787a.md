# TOPIC: OUR LEADERS IN THE DISTRICT
## SUB-TOPIC: DECENTRALISATION — PART B

### Remember
- Primary health care is a decentralised sector.
- Primary education is a decentralised sector.
- Agriculture is a decentralised sector.
- Local leaders settle community disputes.
- Local councils maintain law and order.
- Local leaders implement government policies.
- Decentralisation can increase government expenditure.
- Equal provision of services can become difficult.
