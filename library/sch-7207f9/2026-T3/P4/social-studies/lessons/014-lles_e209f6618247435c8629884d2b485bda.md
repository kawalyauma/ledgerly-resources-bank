# TOPIC: OUR LEADERS IN THE DISTRICT
## SUB-TOPIC: ROLES OF DISTRICT OFFICIALS — PART B

### Remember
- The district council plans development projects.
- The district chairperson supervises district officials.
- The RDC represents the president in the district.
- The district speaker chairs district council meetings.
- The DDHS supervises district health workers.
- The DEO supervises district education programmes.
- Agricultural officers advise crop farmers.
- Veterinary officers advise livestock farmers.
- District police officers maintain law and order.
- District water officers manage clean-water distribution.
- District councils help to settle disputes.
- Local leaders implement government policies.
