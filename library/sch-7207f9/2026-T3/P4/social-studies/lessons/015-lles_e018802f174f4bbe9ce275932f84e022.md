# TOPIC: OUR LEADERS IN THE DISTRICT
## SUB-TOPIC: QUALITIES OF A GOOD LEADER

### Qualities of a good leader
- A good leader should be able and competent.
- A good leader should be kind to others.
- A good leader should respect other people.
- A good leader should be knowledgeable.
- A good leader should be approachable.
- A good leader should be responsible.
- A good leader should be hardworking.

### Other qualities expected from leaders
- A good leader should be tolerant.
- A good leader should obey lawful instructions.
- A good leader should be careful.
- A good leader should cooperate with others.
- A good leader should set a good example.
- A good leader should be confident.

### Ways in which leaders attain authority
- Political leaders gain office through elections.
- Civic leaders gain office through appointment.
- Cultural leaders may gain office through inheritance.
- Voluntary leaders offer service through volunteering.
- Religious leaders serve their faith communities.
- Different types of leaders have different responsibilities.

### Why good leadership matters
- Good leaders help settle community disputes.
- Good leaders help maintain law and order.
- Good leaders plan community development.
- Good leaders promote unity among people.
- Good leaders implement government policies.
- Good leaders protect people and property.
