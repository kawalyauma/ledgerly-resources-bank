# TOPIC: OUR LEADERS IN THE DISTRICT
## SUB-TOPIC: RIGHTS OF DISTRICT RESIDENTS

### Meaning and examples of children's rights
- Children's rights are basic natural freedoms.
- Every child has the right to medical care.
- Children have the right to play.
- Children have the right to protection.
- Every child has the right to a name.
- Children have a right to adequate food.
- Children have a right to education.

### Importance of children's rights
- Children's rights protect them from harm.
- These rights promote respect for children.
- Children's rights support their welfare.
- The LC I vice chairperson protects children's rights.
- Protecting children helps improve community welfare.

### Forms of violations of children's rights
- Child abuse means violating children's rights.
- Physical child abuse is one form of abuse.
- Emotional abuse can violate children's rights.
- Psychological abuse can harm children.
- Protecting children requires respect for their rights.

### Ways to protect human rights
- Citizens should respect other people's rights.
- Citizens can report cases of rights abuse.
- Citizens can support police against rights violations.
- Citizens can educate others about their rights.
- The Uganda Human Rights Commission protects human rights.
- Communities benefit from peace and security.
