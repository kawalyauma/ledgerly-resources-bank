# TOPIC: OUR LEADERS IN THE DISTRICT
## SUB-TOPIC: MEMBERS OF THE DISTRICT COUNCIL — PART B

### Remember the district council
- The district council has elected councillors.
- The district chairperson heads the district politically.
- The council includes a speaker and deputy speaker.
- The council has a vice chairperson.
- The council includes youth representatives.
- People with disabilities have representatives.
- Councillors represent divisions and sub-counties.
- The speaker chairs council meetings.
