# TOPIC: OUR LEADERS IN THE DISTRICT
## SUB-TOPIC: DEPARTMENTS IN THE DISTRICT — PART B

### Remember
- The district health department supervises medical workers.
- The education department oversees school programmes.
- The veterinary department helps livestock farmers.
- The agricultural department assists crop farmers.
- The forestry department conserves forest resources.
- The fisheries department monitors fishing activities.
- The district water office supports clean-water supply.
- The district police protect law and order.
- The district engineer works on roads and bridges.
- The finance office manages expenditure of public funds.
