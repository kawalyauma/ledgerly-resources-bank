# TOPIC: OUR LEADERS IN THE DISTRICT
## SUB-TOPIC: TYPES OF DISTRICT LEADERS — PART B

### Remember the types of district leaders
- Political leaders are elected by citizens.
- Civic leaders gain office by appointment.
- Political leaders include mayors and councillors.
- Civic leaders include district education officers.
- The CAO heads all district civil servants.
- Voluntary leaders provide service in organisations.
- Religious leaders guide communities in religion.
- Traditional leaders belong to cultural institutions.
