# TOPIC: OUR LEADERS IN THE DISTRICT
## SUB-TOPIC: MEMBERS OF THE DISTRICT COUNCIL

### Members of a district council
- The district council has a chairperson.
- The district council has a vice chairperson.
- There are councillors for the youths.
- There are councillors for people with disabilities.
- Councillors represent sub-counties, divisions and towns.
- The district council has a secretary.
- The district council has a speaker.
- The district council has a deputy speaker.

### Work of the district council
- The district council plans development projects.
- It governs local councils in the district.
- The district chairperson coordinates council activities.
- The district chairperson supervises district officials.
- The chairperson monitors district administration.

### Duties of district officials
- The speaker presides over district council meetings.
- The district chairperson supervises local officials.
- The district secretary assists administration.
- Councillors represent people from their areas.
- The district council supports local government work.
- The RDC coordinates central-government programmes.
