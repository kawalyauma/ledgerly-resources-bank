# TOPIC: OUR LEADERS IN THE DISTRICT
## SUB-TOPIC: DEPARTMENTS IN THE DISTRICT

### District administration and civil service
- The Chief Administrative Officer heads district civil servants.
- District civil servants are appointed to provide public services.
- The CAO ensures payment of district civil servants' salaries.
- District officers implement government programmes.
- The district chairperson coordinates local-government activities.

### District health and education departments
- The DDHS is responsible for district medical programmes.
- The DDHS supervises medical workers and health centres.
- The DDHS helps plan responses to disease outbreaks.
- The DEO heads district education departments and institutions.
- The DEO ensures education programmes are carried out.
- The DEO works to improve education standards.

### District agricultural and livestock departments
- The District Agricultural Officer advises crop farmers.
- The Agricultural Officer teaches better farming methods.
- The Agricultural Officer encourages food crop growing.
- The District Veterinary Officer advises livestock farmers.
- The Veterinary Officer supervises veterinary workers.
- The Veterinary Officer teaches better animal care.

### Other important district departments
- The District Forestry Officer promotes forest conservation.
- The District Fisheries Officer controls illegal fishing.
- The District Water Officer manages clean-water distribution.
- The District Police Commander maintains law and order.
- The District Engineer helps build roads and bridges.
- The District Sports Officer manages district sports activities.
