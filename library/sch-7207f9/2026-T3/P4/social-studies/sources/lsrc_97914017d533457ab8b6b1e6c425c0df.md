Terms commonly used in farming
Mixed farming
This is the growing of crops and rearing of animals on the same piece of land at the same time.
Subsistence farming
The growing of crops and rearing of animals mainly for home use/consumption.
Dairy farming
This is the rearing of cattle mainly for milk production.
Ranching
19
This is the rearing of cattle mainly for beef production.
Plantation farming
This is the growing of one perennial crop on a large scale e.g. tea, sugar cane, coffee, etc
Horticulture
This is the growing of vegetables, flowers, and fruits on a large scale.
Floriculture
This is the growing of flowers for sale.
Arable farming
The growing of crops only.
