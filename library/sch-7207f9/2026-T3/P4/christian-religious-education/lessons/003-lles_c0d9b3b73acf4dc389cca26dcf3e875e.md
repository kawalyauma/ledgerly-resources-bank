# TOPIC: RECONCILIATION
## SUB-TOPIC: Week 1 review — Reconciliation
### Summary: Christian ways of reconciling with God
- **Reconciliation** is the restoration of broken relationship between two or more disagreeing parties.
- Believing in Jesus Christ.
- Accepting Jesus as a personal saviour.
- Repenting for our sins.
- Asking for forgiveness.
- Jesus died on the cross for our sins.
- Through acceptance of Christ’s salvation, we become daughters and sons of God.
### Summary: Practice: Christian ways of reconciling with God
- No person can ever get peace unless he is reconciled with God.
- Christians must always seek peace with others and God.
- To have peace of God, we must have faith in Him and Jesus Christ.
- Reconciliation restores friendship and love.
- It restores personal communications.
- It prevents bitterness and revenge.
- It promotes peace and harmony.
