# TOPIC: EXPRESSION OF THE FUTURE
## SUB-TOPIC: Reading: Akot’s plan to go to the zoo

### New words
- zoo, chimpanzees, monkeys, pancakes, evening.

### Akot’s plan to go to the zoo
- Next Sunday, my sister Joyce and I will visit the zoo.
- We shall see many animals and birds.
- We shall see lions, chimpanzees and monkeys.
- We shall not see elephants because they are not in the zoo.
- On our way, we shall eat pancakes and drink soda.
- We shall take bananas for chimpanzees and monkeys.

### Returning home
- We shall come back home in the evening, very tired.
- We shall bathe with warm water.
- After that, we shall go to bed to rest.
- We shall wake up early and go to school the next day.

### Example: answering in a full sentence
- When will Akot go to the zoo?
- Akot will go to the zoo next Sunday.

### Exercise
1. With whom will Akot go to the zoo?
2. What will they eat on their way to the zoo?
3. Why will they not see the elephants at the zoo?
4. What will they take for the chimpanzees and the monkeys?
5. How many people will go to the zoo next Sunday?
