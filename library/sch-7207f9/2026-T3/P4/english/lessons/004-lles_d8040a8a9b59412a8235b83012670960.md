# TOPIC: EXPRESSION OF THE FUTURE
## SUB-TOPIC: Future-time vocabulary

### New words
- tomorrow, next week, next month, next year, shan’t, won’t.

### Future-time words
- tomorrow
- next week
- next month
- next year

### Negative forms
- shall not
- will not
- shan’t
- won’t

### Example 1
- Jane will go to school tomorrow.
- We shall not go to the market next week.

### Example 2
- We shall not play with him.
- We shall go with him.
- We shall see lions, chimpanzees and monkeys.
- We shall come back home in the evening.

### Exercise
1. Construct a meaningful sentence using tomorrow.
2. Construct a meaningful sentence using next week.
3. Use the sentence table to write five sentences with shall, shall not, will or will not.
