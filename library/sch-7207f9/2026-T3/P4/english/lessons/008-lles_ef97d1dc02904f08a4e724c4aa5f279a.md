# TOPIC: EXPRESSION OF THE FUTURE
## SUB-TOPIC: Using before

### New words
- before, first action, second action, breakfast, homework.

### Using before
- I wash my hands before eating food.
- First action: washing hands.
- Second action: eating food.

### Example 1
- She will do her homework before going to play.
- First action: doing homework.
- Second action: going to play.

### Example 2
- Before going to pray, Musa will take breakfast.
- First action: taking breakfast.
- Second action: going to pray.

### Example 3: before in the middle
- I wash my hands before eating food.
- She will do her homework before going to play.

### Exercise
1. Complete the source sentence: I wash my hands ___ eating food.
2. Complete the source sentence: She will do her homework ___ going to play.
3. Punctuate: before going to pray musa will take breakfast
