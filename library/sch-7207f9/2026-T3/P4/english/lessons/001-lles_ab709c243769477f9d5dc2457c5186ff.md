# TOPIC: EXPRESSION OF THE FUTURE
## SUB-TOPIC: Using if

### New words
- if, condition, main clause, present simple, future simple.

### Conditional clauses
- If and unless introduce a conditional clause.
- The if-clause expresses a condition.
- The main clause commonly uses the future simple tense.
- The if-clause commonly uses the present simple tense.

### Punctuation
- A comma follows the if-clause when it comes first.

### Example 1
- If I get money, I will buy a new radio.
- If-clause: If I get money.
- Main clause: I will buy a new radio.

### Example 2
- Mother will punish us if we don’t complete the work.
- If I pass exams, I will fly to London.
