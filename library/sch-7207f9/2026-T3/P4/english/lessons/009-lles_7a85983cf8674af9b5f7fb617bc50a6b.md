# TOPIC: EXPRESSION OF THE FUTURE
## SUB-TOPIC: Using after

### New words
- after, past perfect, past simple, finished, homework.

### Using after
- After shows that one action follows another.
- For these completed past actions, the first action uses past perfect.
- The second action uses past simple.

### Example 1: separate sentences
- Tom finished his homework.
- He went to sleep.
- The homework was finished first.
- Going to sleep happened second.

### Example 2: after at the beginning
- After Tom had finished his homework, he went to sleep.
- First action: had finished his homework.
- Second action: went to sleep.

### Example 3: after in the middle
- Tom went to sleep after he had finished his homework.
- The sentence keeps the same order of events.

### Exercise
1. Join using After at the beginning: Tom finished his homework. He went to sleep.
2. Join using after in the middle: Tom finished his homework. He went to sleep.
3. Punctuate: after tom had finished his homework he went to sleep
