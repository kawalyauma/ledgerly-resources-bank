# TOPIC: FIQH — CONGREGATIONAL PRAYER AND CONDUCT OF PRAYER
## SUB-TOPIC: Week 1 review
### Summary: Requirements for congregational prayer
- **Congregational prayer** is prayer performed by more than one person led by an Imam.
- Another name for congregational prayer is group prayer.
- Prophet Muhammad (P.B.U.H) said group prayer is better by twenty-seven times.
- Juma prayer, Tarawiih prayer, Idd prayer and funeral prayer are examples.
- The five daily prayers can be performed in a group.
- Requirements include being sane and having the right intention.
- Requirements include ablution, clean dress and decent dress.
- Muslims face the Qibla while praying.
### Summary: Conduct of congregational prayer
- **Imaam** is the person who leads Muslims in prayer.
- **Ma-amuum** is one who follows the Imam in prayer.
- The Imam leads the congregational prayer.
- The Ma-amuum follows the Imam strictly.
- Juma prayer has two rakahs.
- Idd-Al-Fitr prayer and Idd-Al-Adhuha prayer have two rakahs each.
- Tarawiih has eleven rakahs.
- Swalaatul-Janazah is the funeral prayer.
- The five daily prayers are Subuhi, Zuhri, Asr, Magharib and Isha’i.
