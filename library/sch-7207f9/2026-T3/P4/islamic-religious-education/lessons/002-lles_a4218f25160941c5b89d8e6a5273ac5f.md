# TOPIC: FIQH — CONGREGATIONAL PRAYER AND CONDUCT OF PRAYER
## SUB-TOPIC: Conduct of congregational prayer
### New words
Imaam, Ma-amuum, Tak-biiratul-Ihram, Rakah, Sujuud
### Meaning of words
- **Imaam** is the person who leads Muslims in prayer.
- **Ma-amuum** is one who follows the Imam in prayer.
- **Tak-biiratul-Ihram** is saying Allah-Akbar when beginning a prayer.
- **Rakah** is a Swalat round or unit of prayer.
- **Sujuud** is putting your head on the ground during prayer.
### Roles in congregational prayer
- The Imam leads the congregational prayer.
- The Ma-amuum follows the Imam strictly.
### Congregational prayers and rakahs
- Juma prayer has two rakahs.
- Idd-Al-Fitr prayer has two rakahs.
- Idd-Al-Adhuha prayer has two rakahs.
- Tarawiih has eleven rakahs.
- Swalaatul-Janazah is the funeral prayer.
### Five daily prayers
- Subuhi
- Zuhri
- Asr
- Magharib
- Isha’i
### Note
- The Imam leads and the Ma-amuum follows the Imam’s actions.
- Group prayer shows equality before Allah and respect for one another.
- Group prayer makes friends and lets people share ideas.
