# TOPIC: FIQH — CONGREGATIONAL PRAYER AND CONDUCT OF PRAYER
## SUB-TOPIC: Requirements for congregational prayer
### New words
Congregation/group prayer, Qibla, Wudhu/ablution
### Meaning of congregational prayer
- **Congregational prayer** is prayer performed by more than one person led by an Imam.
- Another name for congregational prayer is group prayer.
- Prophet Muhammad (P.B.U.H) said group prayer is better by twenty-seven times.
### Examples of congregational prayers
- Juma prayer
- Tarawiih prayer
- Idd prayer
- Funeral prayer
- The five daily prayers can be performed in a group.
### Requirements for congregational prayer
- Be sane.
- Have the right intention.
- Perform ablution before prayer.
- Wear clean dress.
- Dress decently.
- Face the Qibla.
### Note
- Qibla is the direction Muslims face while praying.
- Wudhu is cleaning some parts of the body before prayer.
- A group prayer is led by an Imam.
