# TOPIC: COMMUNICABLE INTESTINAL DISEASES AND WORM INFESTATION
## SUB-TOPIC: Oral rehydration solution

### New words
- dehydration, rehydration, solute, solvent, dissolves

### Meaning
- ORS means Oral Rehydration Solution.
- ORS helps replace lost body fluids.
- Rehydration is the replacement of lost body fluids.
- SSS means Salt Sugar Solution.
- SSS can be made locally at home.
- ORS is made in factories.

### Materials for preparing ORS
- One sachet of ORS (oral rehydration salts)
- One litre of drinking water
- Spoon
- Clean container

### Preparing SSS
- Wash hands with water and soap.
- Measure one litre of clean, cool boiled water.
- Pour the water into a clean container.
- Measure eight levelled teaspoons of sugar.
- Measure one levelled teaspoon of salt.
- Add the sugar and salt into the water.
- Stir until the salt and sugar dissolve completely.

### Importance of ORS and SSS
- ORS and SSS replace lost body fluids.
- The solutions help replace lost water, salts and energy.

### Note
- Use safe or clean boiled water for the solution.
- Wash hands with water and soap before preparation.
