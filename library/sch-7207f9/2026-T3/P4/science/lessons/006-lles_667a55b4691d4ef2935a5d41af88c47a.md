# TOPIC: COMMUNICABLE INTESTINAL DISEASES
## SUB-TOPIC: TYPHOID AND ITS PREVENTION

### Meaning and cause
- Typhoid is a communicable intestinal disease.
- Typhoid fever is caused by bacteria.
- The bacterium is called Salmonella typhi.
- Typhoid germs can contaminate food.
- Typhoid germs can contaminate drinking water.

### How typhoid spreads
- Typhoid can spread through contaminated food.
- Typhoid can spread through contaminated water.
- Houseflies may carry germs onto exposed food.
- Eating food with germs may cause infection.
- Poor sanitation allows intestinal germs to spread.

### Signs and symptoms of typhoid
- A person with typhoid may suffer diarrhoea.
- Fever is one symptom of typhoid.
- Headache can also occur in typhoid illness.
- Typhoid affects infected people's health.
- Typhoid can cause weakness and sickness.

### Preventing typhoid
- Use clean and safe water for drinking.
- Wash hands after using the latrine.
- Dispose of faeces safely in toilets.
- Keep food covered against flies.
- Maintain proper household sanitation.
