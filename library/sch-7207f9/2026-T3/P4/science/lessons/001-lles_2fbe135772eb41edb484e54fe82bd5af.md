# TOPIC: COMMUNICABLE INTESTINAL DISEASES
## SUB-TOPIC: COMMUNICABLE DISEASES

### Meaning of communicable diseases
- Communicable diseases can pass from infected to healthy people.
- Communicable diseases are also called infectious diseases.
- They are spread by disease-causing germs.
- Some germs are transmitted through contaminated food.

### Examples of communicable diseases
- Diarrhoea is a communicable intestinal disease.
- Dysentery is another communicable intestinal disease.
- Cholera spreads through infected food and water.
- Typhoid can spread through contaminated food and water.
- Some communicable diseases are caused by bacteria.
- Other infections can be caused by viruses.

### Ways germs spread
- Infected faeces may carry germs.
- Flies can carry germs to uncovered food.
- Dirty fingers can contaminate food.
- Drinking unsafe water can introduce germs.
- Eating contaminated food can cause disease.

### Preventing spread of germs
- Use latrines to dispose of faeces.
- Wash hands using clean water and soap.
- Cover food to prevent flies reaching it.
- Keep the surroundings and water sources clean.
- Destroy housefly breeding places where possible.
