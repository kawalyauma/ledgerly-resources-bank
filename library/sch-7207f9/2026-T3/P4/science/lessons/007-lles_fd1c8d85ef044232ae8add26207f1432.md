# TOPIC: COMMUNICABLE INTESTINAL DISEASES AND WORM INFESTATION
## SUB-TOPIC: Dehydration

### Meaning
- **Dehydration** is when the body does not have enough water and mineral salts.
- A dehydrated person loses water, energy and salts.
- Lost salts include sodium and potassium.

### Causes of dehydration
- Severe diarrhoea
- Severe vomiting
- Severe sweating

### Signs and symptoms of dehydration
- Sunken eyes
- Dry mouth
- Little or no urine
- Little or no sweat
- A dehydrated baby has a sunken fontanel.
- A pinch on the skin goes slowly to its original position.
- Little or no tears

### Treatment of dehydration
- Give the patient Oral Rehydration Solution (ORS).
- Give the patient Sugar Salt Solution (SSS).
- ORS and SSS replace lost water, salts and energy.
- Rehydration is the replacement of lost body fluids.
- Plenty of water, fruit drinks and soup can be taken.

### Note
- Severe diarrhoea, vomiting and sweating cause dehydration.
- ORS and SSS help replace lost body fluids.
