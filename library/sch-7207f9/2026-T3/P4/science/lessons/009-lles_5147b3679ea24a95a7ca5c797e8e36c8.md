# TOPIC: COMMUNICABLE INTESTINAL DISEASES AND WORM INFESTATION
## SUB-TOPIC: Intestinal worm infestation

### New words
- host, ecto, endo

### Meaning of worms
- Worms are long, slender, soft-bodied animals with no limbs.
- Worms can live in soil, bodies of animals, fresh water, plants, fruits or vegetables.
- Intestinal worms live in the intestine.

### Examples of intestinal worms
- Tapeworms
- Hookworms
- Threadworms
- Ascaris

### How worms enter our bodies
- Eating contaminated food with worms or eggs
- Drinking contaminated water
- Eating undercooked food

### Why worms are parasites
- A parasite lives in or on another living thing.
- A parasite gets food from its host.
- Intestinal worms get food from the body they live in.

### Note
- Worms have no limbs.
- Contaminated food or water can carry worms or eggs into the body.
