The meaning of Trinity
The word trinity comes from a Latin word Trinitas
Trinitas
 Trinity means Trinitas. It means that although God is one, he exists in three persons
namely.
 God the father
 God the son
 God the Holy Spirit
When the three persons are put together, we call that trinity.
6
Examples in the Bible which show God referring to the three persons
God uses “us” “our” not “me” or my
1. In genesis 1:26, God said let us make man in our image after our likeness. God didn’t say
let me make man in my image.
2. In Genesis 11:7, God said “come let us go down and mix their languages.
3. Jesus told his disciple to baptize in the name of the father, the son and the Holy Spirit.
 All the three persons are fully divine. This means that every person is equal in glory,
power, and substance.
 Each is everlasting, every where, knows very thing an has unlimited powers.
 Christians apportion each person with a certain work. The work of the creation was the
father, the work of creation is for the father, and the work of sanctification is for the Holy
Spirit.
 No single works is done without involvement of the three persons.
