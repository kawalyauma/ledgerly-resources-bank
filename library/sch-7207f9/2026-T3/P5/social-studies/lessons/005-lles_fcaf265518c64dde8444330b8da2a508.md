# TOPIC: UGANDA AS AN INDEPENDENT NATION
## SUB-TOPIC: THE NATIONAL MOTTO AND THE COAT OF ARMS

### Remember
- Uganda's national motto is For God and My Country.
- The motto encourages nationalism, patriotism and national unity.
- The motto reflects Ugandans' belief in God.
- The crested crane represents gentleness and peacefulness.
- The Uganda kob represents the country's wildlife.
- Coffee and cotton show Uganda's agricultural heritage.
- The drum represents culture and traditional communication.
- The blue stripes represent Uganda's lakes and the River Nile.
