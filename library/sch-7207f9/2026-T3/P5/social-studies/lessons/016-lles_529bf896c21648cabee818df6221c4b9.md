# TOPIC: THE GOVERNMENT OF UGANDA
## SUB-TOPIC: Practice: duties of the government and communication

### Remember
1. **Government** provides services, maintains security, constructs infrastructure and pays its workers.
2. **Social services** improve people's way of living, such as education, water, transport and security.
3. **Social service centres** include schools, hospitals, banks, police stations and post offices.
4. **Communication** means sending and receiving messages.
5. Traditional communication includes drums, horns, whistles and messengers.
6. Modern communication includes radio, television, telephones and newspapers.
7. Communication promotes trade, national unity and peace.
8. Problems include lack of funds, insecurity, illiteracy and poor communication networks.

### Exercise
1. Write down four ways to show how people benefit from the government.
2. Mention any two social services provided by the post office.
3. Name any two means of communication that are commonly used at a post office.
4. Give any three ways a post office is important to people in your community.
5. Give any three ways the police force is important to people in your district.
