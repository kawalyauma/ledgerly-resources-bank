## SUB-TOPIC: RESPONSIBILITIES OF A CITIZEN

### Meaning and key facts about responsibilities of a citizen
- Briefly give the meaning of a citizen
- Point out two ways one can become a citizen of Uganda.
- By denouncing the citizenship. Responsibilities/duties/obligations of a citizen
- To protect the citizens and their property through the army.
- To provide social services like education to the citizens.
- Keeps law and order.
- Pays tax promptly
- Preserves his environment
- Respects and preserves the rights and freedom of others

### Features and related facts about responsibilities of a citizen
- Upholds the national constitution
- Respects the nation’s attributes.
- Renders national services whenever they are required.
- Participates in the national activities which promote democracy and good governance e.g. general elections
- Participate in self help projects. Activity
- To provide and guide development programme to all areas of the country.
- Citizenship by birth
- A person born by parents who are Ugandans becomes a citizen of Uganda by birth.
- Citizenship by registration

### Further details about responsibilities of a citizen
- A person married to a Uganda citizen for at least a period of three years.
- Citizenship by Naturalization
- This is where a person from another country is made a citizen of a specified country after spending many years (20years)
- Citizenship by descent
- Right to access public information Responsibilities of citizens Define the following
- If a person becomes a citizen of another country
- To respect the rights of other citizens
- Through descent (is the legal right of belonging to a country a person gets when his / her ancestors were citizens in that country)
- Single citizenship. This is the legal right of belonging to one particular country.
