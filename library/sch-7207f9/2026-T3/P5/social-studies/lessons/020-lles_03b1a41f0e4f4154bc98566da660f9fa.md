# TOPIC: THE GOVERNMENT OF UGANDA
## SUB-TOPIC: Practice: revenue, expenditure and budgets

### Remember
1. **Revenue** is money government receives from sources such as taxes, tourism, grants and court fines.
2. **Taxation** is the major source of government income in Uganda.
3. **Direct taxes** include income tax and PAYE.
4. **Indirect taxes** include VAT, customs duty and excise duty.
5. The **URA** collects taxes and works to control smuggling.
6. **Expenditure** means the money government spends on services and programmes.
7. A **budget** estimates income and expenditure during a period.
8. **Surplus** means income exceeds spending; **balanced** means equal amounts; **deficit** means spending exceeds income.
