# TOPIC: UGANDA AS AN INDEPENDENT NATION
## SUB-TOPIC: THE NATIONAL ANTHEM

### Meaning and history of the national anthem
- The national anthem is Uganda's official national song.
- It was composed by George Wilberforce Kakoma.
- The anthem was first officially performed on Independence Day.
- Uganda's anthem has three stanzas.
- It expresses the country's principles, hopes and goals.
- The national anthem promotes unity and patriotism.

### Messages in Uganda's national anthem
- The first stanza expresses belief in God.
- It calls on Ugandans to remain united and free.
- The second stanza speaks about peace and friendship.
- It expresses love and labour for the country.
- The third stanza describes fertile soil and sunshine.
- It reminds people of Uganda's rich natural environment.

### Occasions when the national anthem is performed
- It is performed on school assemblies.
- It is performed during parliamentary sessions.
- It is performed at official ceremonies.
- It is performed at the opening of sports activities.
- It is performed during important public functions.

### Respect and importance of the anthem
- People stand at attention during the national anthem.
- They remain still until its performance ends.
- The anthem promotes respect for Uganda.
- It encourages national identity and unity.
- It reminds citizens of their national duties.
- It also serves as a national prayer.

### Note
- George Wilberforce Kakoma composed the anthem.
- The anthem has three stanzas.
- Respect is shown by standing at attention.
