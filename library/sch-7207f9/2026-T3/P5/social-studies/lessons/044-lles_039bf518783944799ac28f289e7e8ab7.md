# TOPIC: POPULATION, SIZE AND DISTRIBUTION
## SUB-TOPIC: Practice: HIV/AIDS and population

### Remember
1. **HIV** means Human Immunodeficiency Virus.
2. **AIDS** means Acquired Immune Deficiency Syndrome.
3. The source identifies sharing infected sharp objects and infected blood contact as transmission risks.
4. The prevention message in the textbook uses **A**, **B** and **C**.
5. **TASO** is named as a provider of counselling, antiretroviral medicine and food relief.
6. Social effects of AIDS include loss of life, isolation and children becoming orphans.
7. Economic effects include a loss of workers, less efficiency at work and a reduced market for goods.
8. Affected families can face poverty associated with treatment expenses.

### Population and family effects
The same illness can have effects on families and on the economy. The source lists bereavement, isolation and orphanhood as social effects. It also records a reduced labour supply and a reduced market for goods because of deaths. Families may experience financial difficulties due to money spent on treatment. These points show how a public health issue can affect the number of workers and the lives of children and adults in a community.

### Support and prevention recap
TASO is the support organisation identified in the resource. It is described as providing counselling, ARVs and food relief. The source also identifies prevention of infection through avoiding risky exposure to infected blood, and lists the ABC message.
