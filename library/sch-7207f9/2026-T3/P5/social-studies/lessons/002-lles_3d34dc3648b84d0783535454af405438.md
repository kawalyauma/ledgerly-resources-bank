# TOPIC: THE GOVERNMENT OF UGANDA
## SUB-TOPIC: Practice: government, local government and decentralisation

### Remember
1. **Government** is the body that manages public affairs in a country.
2. **Central government** handles national affairs while **local government** handles local affairs.
3. Local councils are organised from village LC I, parish LC II, sub-county LC III and county LC IV to district LC V.
4. **Decentralisation** transfers some powers to local authorities.
5. Decentralisation helps bring services near the people and can make administration easier.
6. **By-laws** are laws made by local councils; they help to maintain order and peace.
7. Central government maintains major roads, while local government maintains feeder roads.
