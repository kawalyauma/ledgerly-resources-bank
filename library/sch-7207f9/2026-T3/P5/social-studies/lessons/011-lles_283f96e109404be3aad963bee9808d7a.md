### Features and related facts about citizenship
- Refers to a strong love one has for one’s country. A patriotic citizen is a person who has or shows great love for his / her country.
- Through descent (is the legal right of belonging to a country a person gets when his / her ancestors were citizens in that country)
- A Passport is an official document issued to citizens allowing them to travel abroad and enter the home country.
- A National ID card is a legal document given to all Ugandan citizens for easy identification.
- A National ID shows national identity and also enables citizens to get access to the social services provided in the country.
- By descent Conditions under which a person can lose citizenship
- By forging Ugandan citizenship
- A person married to a Uganda citizen for at least a period of three years.
- This is where a person from another country is made a citizen of a specified country after spending many years (20years)

### Further facts about citizenship
- To protect the citizens and their property through the army.
- To provide social services like education to the citizens.
- A person born by parents who are Ugandans becomes a citizen of Uganda by birth.
- Citizenship by adoption
- Citizenship by foundling
- A foundling is an abandoned child of unknown parents and is found in Uganda. Loss of citizenship
