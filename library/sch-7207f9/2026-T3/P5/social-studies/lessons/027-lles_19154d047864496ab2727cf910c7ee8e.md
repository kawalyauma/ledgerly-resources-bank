# TOPIC: POPULATION SIZE AND DISTRIBUTION
## SUB-TOPIC: Practice: population census

### Remember
1. **Population census** means an official count of the population, together with information about them.
2. **Enumerators** are trained people who collect household information.
3. The **Uganda Bureau of Statistics (UBOS)** carries out census in Uganda.
4. Census helps government plan for the people and know their numbers.
5. Data collected includes age, sex, housing, education and occupation.
6. The **census night** is the night before the count takes place.
7. Poor roads, insecurity and bad weather can make census difficult.
8. The source notes describe an interval of ten years between national censuses.
