# TOPIC: THE GOVERNMENT OF UGANDA
## SUB-TOPIC: The Uganda Revenue Authority, why people pay taxes and smuggling

### The Uganda Revenue Authority
The **Uganda Revenue Authority (URA)** is the government body responsible for collecting taxes in Uganda. The supplied notes identify its roles as collecting taxes, assessing taxpayers, educating people about taxes and helping control smuggling. The authority therefore helps the government get income needed for public work. Tax assessment involves determining the tax to be paid. Tax education helps people understand why they are required to contribute. The government uses money collected through the tax system for services and programmes.

### Why people pay taxes
Taxes help the government raise money to support services and national programmes. The notes give payment of civil servants and provision of social services as reasons people should pay taxes. Tax revenue can help government operate schools, hospitals and maintain roads. Some people fail to pay taxes because they do not understand tax requirements, tax rates are high or because of corruption among some revenue officers. Tax education and fair administration can help make the collection of revenue more effective.

### Meaning and effects of smuggling
**Smuggling** is the illegal importation or exportation of goods. It is a problem for tax collection because goods may cross borders without the correct taxes being paid. The notes say smuggling reduces tax income and can bring poor-quality or expired goods into the country. It may also reduce the market for locally manufactured goods. These effects make smuggling harmful to government revenue and the economy. Controlling smuggling is one of the duties associated with revenue protection.

### Problems and ways of controlling smuggling
Other problems faced by URA include tax evasion, tax defaulting, false declarations and corrupt revenue officers. Suggested solutions are tax education, fair tax rates, employment of honest officers and punishment of corrupt officers. Smuggling may be reduced through the enforcement of laws against illegal trade. Revenue officers can also explain the importance of paying taxes. The purpose of these measures is to improve revenue collection so that government has funds for its services and the development of the country.
