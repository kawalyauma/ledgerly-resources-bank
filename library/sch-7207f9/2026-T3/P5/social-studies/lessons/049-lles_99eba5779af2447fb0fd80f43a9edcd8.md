# TOPIC: POPULATION, SIZE AND DISTRIBUTION
## SUB-TOPIC: Practice: topical test on population, size and distribution

### Remember
1. **Population** is the total number of people living in an area.
2. **Population density** is the number of people per square kilometre.
3. **Population distribution** means the way people are spread over an area.
4. **Population growth** is an increase in the number of people.
5. **Population explosion** is a sudden increase in that number.
6. A **population census** counts the people living in an area.
7. **Population structure** describes age and sex.
8. **Optimum population** means the available resources support the number of people.

### Factors and settlement
Population distribution is influenced by **rainfall**, **soil fertility**, **employment opportunities**, **social services**, security and relief. Areas with reliable rainfall and suitable land often attract residents. Places with poor soils, insecurity or limited services may have few people. Densely settled places can enjoy a larger market and more workers, but they can also experience pressure on land, food and public services.
