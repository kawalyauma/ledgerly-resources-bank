# TOPIC: THE GOVERNMENT OF UGANDA
## SUB-TOPIC: Practice: the Legislature

### Remember
1. The **Legislature** is the law-making arm of government and is also known as Parliament.
2. The **Speaker** chairs parliamentary debates; the **Deputy Speaker** acts when the Speaker is absent.
3. The **Clerk** records Parliament's proceedings, while the **Sergeant at Arms** keeps order and security.
4. **Hansard** is the record of parliamentary proceedings.
5. A **bill** is a proposed law; an **Act** is a law passed and signed by the President.
6. The **mace** represents the authority of the Speaker and Parliament.
7. Parliament approves the national budget and checks government expenditure.
8. MPs may represent constituencies or special interest groups.
