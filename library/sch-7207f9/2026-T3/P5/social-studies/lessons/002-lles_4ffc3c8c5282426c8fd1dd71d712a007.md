# TOPIC: UGANDA AS AN INDEPENDENT NATION
## SUB-TOPIC: THE NATIONAL FLAG

### Symbols of an independent nation
- National symbols are features that identify a particular country.
- Uganda's national symbols include the national flag.
- Other symbols include the national anthem and coat of arms.
- The national motto and constitution are also national symbols.

### The Uganda national flag
[[diagram:uganda-flag-p5-week1]]
- The national flag identifies Uganda as an independent nation.
- Its main colours are black, yellow and red.
- Black represents the black African people of Uganda.
- Yellow represents Uganda's abundant sunshine.
- Red represents international brotherhood among people.
- The white circle represents peace.
- The circle contains Uganda's crested crane emblem.
- Grace Ibingira designed Uganda's national flag.
- The national flag first replaced the Union Jack in 1962.

### Places and occasions for displaying the flag
- The national flag is flown at schools.
- It is flown at government offices.
- It is flown at airports and police stations.
- It is flown at embassies and high commissions.
- The flag identifies Uganda at important public events.
- Half-mast flying may mark national mourning.

### Ways of respecting the national flag
- People stand still while the flag is raised or lowered.
- The flag must not touch the ground.
- The flag should not be used as clothing.
- The flag should not be used for commercial advertising.
- The crested crane faces the flagpole when the flag is raised.

### Note
- The national flag promotes national identity and unity.
- Its three main colours are black, yellow and red.
