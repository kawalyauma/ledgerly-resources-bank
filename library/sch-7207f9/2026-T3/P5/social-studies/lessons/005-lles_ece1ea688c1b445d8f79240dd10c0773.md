# TOPIC: THE GOVERNMENT OF UGANDA
## SUB-TOPIC: Practice: the Executive

### Remember
1. The **Executive** is the administrative organ of government.
2. The **President** heads the Executive and is commander-in-chief of the armed forces.
3. The President chairs Cabinet meetings, appoints senior officials and may pardon deserving offenders.
4. The **Cabinet** works through government ministers.
5. Ministers lead ministries and ensure government policies are implemented.
6. **Civil servants** carry out government programmes and daily public work.
7. The **Permanent Secretary** heads civil servants in a ministry, while the **Chief Administrative Officer** heads them in a district.
8. The President receives instruments of power at the swearing-in ceremony.

### Exercise
1. Mention any three instruments of power given to the president on the swearing in ceremony.
2. How does the chief justice help the president to attain leadership powers?
3. What role do civil servants play in government?
4. What title is given to the; (i) Political head of a ministry? (ii) Highest civil servant in a ministry? (iii) Highest civil servant in a district?
5. State any three roles performed by the government.
