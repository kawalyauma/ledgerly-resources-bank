# TOPIC: THE GOVERNMENT OF UGANDA
## SUB-TOPIC: Practice: the constitution

### Remember
1. The **national constitution** is the supreme set of laws for governing Uganda.
2. **By-laws** are laws made by local government.
3. Uganda's constitutions since independence include the **1962**, **1966**, **1967** and **1995** constitutions.
4. The 1966 Constitution was called the **Pigeon-hole Constitution**.
5. The 1967 Constitution declared Uganda a republic and abolished kingdoms.
6. The 1995 Constitution was made public on **8 October 1995**.
7. A constitution protects rights, guides governance and encourages peaceful leadership changes.
8. It sets conditions for elections and the duties of the Electoral Commission.
