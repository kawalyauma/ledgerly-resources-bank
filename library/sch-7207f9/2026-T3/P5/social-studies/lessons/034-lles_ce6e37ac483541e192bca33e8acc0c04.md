# TOPIC: POPULATION SIZE AND DISTRIBUTION
## SUB-TOPIC: Practice: population distribution

### Remember
1. **Population distribution** is the way people are spread over an area.
2. **Dense population** means many people compared with the size of the land.
3. **Sparse population** means few people compared with land size.
4. Reliable rainfall, fertile soils and good services may attract residents.
5. Jobs, industries and towns may encourage settlement and migration.
6. Drought, steep slopes, disasters and insecurity can discourage settlement.
7. Lakeshores, fertile plateau areas and towns can have dense populations.
8. Dry regions, swampy land and unsafe places may be sparsely populated.
