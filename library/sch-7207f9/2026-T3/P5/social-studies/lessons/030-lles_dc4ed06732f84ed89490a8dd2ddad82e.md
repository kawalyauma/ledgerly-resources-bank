# TOPIC: POPULATION SIZE AND DISTRIBUTION
## SUB-TOPIC: Practice: population growth

### Remember
1. **Population growth** is an increase in the number of people in an area.
2. **Population explosion** is a sudden increase in the population.
3. Growth is influenced by births, deaths, migration, fertility and health services.
4. A larger population can create more labour and a bigger market for goods.
5. Fast growth may cause unemployment, land shortage and slums.
6. Disease may spread faster in crowded settlements.
7. A census provides information government can use to plan for the people.
8. Natural disasters and other hazards can reduce population numbers.
