# TOPIC: THE GOVERNMENT OF UGANDA
## SUB-TOPIC: Meaning of government, central and local government

### Meaning of government
A **government** is a ruling body of a country. It is the group of people who exercise authority over a state and manage its affairs. Government provides public services and keeps order in the country. Uganda needs its government for national development and for organising services such as education, health, transport and security. The central and local authorities share responsibility for serving the people.

### Central and local government
There are two levels of government in Uganda: **central government** and **local government**. Central government manages affairs of the whole nation. Local government manages affairs in districts and other local areas. There is one central government, but there are many local governments. Central government cares for development of the entire country, while local government cares for development of its own locality. Central government maintains major roads; local government maintains feeder roads. Central government runs major hospitals; local government controls health centres.

### Levels of local government
Local councils work at different levels. In a rural area, a **village** has Local Council I (LC I). A **parish** has Local Council II (LC II). A **sub-county** has Local Council III (LC III). A **county** has Local Council IV (LC IV). A **district** has Local Council V (LC V). The political leaders of the councils are called chairpersons. These councils help people take local matters to their leaders at different levels, from the village to the district.

### Decentralisation and government services
**Decentralisation** is the transfer of some powers from central government to local government. It brings social services closer to people and makes administration easier. Examples of decentralised sectors are primary health care, primary education and agriculture. Government also maintains law and order, builds schools and hospitals, constructs roads and defends the country from attack. These duties show why citizens need government. Both the national government and local authorities have a part in providing services, but they operate at different levels.
