# TOPIC: THE GOVERNMENT OF UGANDA
## SUB-TOPIC: Duties of the government and social services

### Duties of government
A **government** has duties towards the people it governs. The source notes say Uganda's government maintains law and order, protects the country against attacks and promotes national development. It makes the national budget, conducts elections and pays salaries to civil servants. Government also constructs and maintains infrastructure and provides services to the people. These duties involve leadership, security and public welfare. National administration and local authorities carry out different tasks so that people can benefit from government programmes in their communities.

### Meaning of social services
**Social services** are activities that improve people's ways of living. They are provided by government, private individuals and non-government organisations. Government services listed in the notes include education, transport, water, banking, security and communication. Health services are also important for people's welfare. Places where services are provided include schools, hospitals, taxi parks, police stations, banks and radio stations. Social services are useful because they help people learn, travel, communicate, remain healthy and have security in daily life. Different centres provide different services.

### Problems faced when providing services
Government can face difficulties while providing services. The source notes identify **shortage of funds**, **corruption**, **insecurity**, **bad weather** and **high population growth**. Other difficulties include illiteracy, poor transport and communication networks and outbreaks of serious diseases. A shortage of funds can make it difficult to run or extend services. Poor roads and communication can make delivery harder. Insecurity and disease outbreaks can also interrupt ordinary public work. Such challenges may affect the places that need public services.

### Ways to improve service provision
The notes suggest several solutions for difficulties facing government services. These include maintaining good security and enforcing laws against corruption. People can be sensitised about the importance of social services. The source also suggests encouraging family planning to manage high population growth. Stronger security and fair administration can help public offices carry out their duties. Government's aim is to make services reach people and support development. Education, health, transport, water and security remain important examples of public services that the country needs.
