# TOPIC: POPULATION SIZE AND DISTRIBUTION
## SUB-TOPIC: Advantages and dangers of fast population growth

### Meaning and effects of growth
**Population growth** means an increasing number of people in an area. When many more people live in a place, this creates a larger market and increases the number of people available to work. It also means that more people require food, shelter and social services. The effects depend on resources and opportunities. Population growth can benefit communities with employment and sufficient services but can cause shortages where housing, land and health care cannot support increasing numbers.

### Benefits of population growth
The notes describe a number of benefits from a large population. More people can provide labour for farms, businesses and other work. A larger population may create a bigger market for goods that are sold in the area. It can also increase the number of people paying taxes to government. These benefits may encourage trade, investment and development. A community with many workers and customers may support more economic activities. Population growth is beneficial when people have opportunities to work and sufficient resources.

### Dangers of rapid growth
When population growth is very fast, services and resources may become insufficient. Problems named in the learning notes include shortage of land, unemployment, development of slums and high crime rates. Many people living close together may increase the spread of communicable diseases. More housing may be needed than can be provided. Land may be divided into small plots in a process called **land fragmentation**. Use of natural resources may also increase, leading to environmental damage.

### Pressure on public services
Fast population growth may make it difficult for authorities to provide enough schools, hospitals, water and roads. Government may need to spend more on these services, leaving fewer resources for other development work. The source notes also mention damage to the environment and pressure on natural resources. Population census information is important for planning for the people and identifying their needs. The advantages and disadvantages of population growth show why planning must consider both the number of residents and the services available.
