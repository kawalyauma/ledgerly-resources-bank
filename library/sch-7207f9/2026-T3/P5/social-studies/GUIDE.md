# Social Studies · Primary Five 2026 · Term 3

- Class level: P5
- Weeks: 10
- Periods per week: 5
- Lesson length: 40 minutes (one period)
- Examples allowed: no (not Mathematics, English or Reading)
- Class depth: P5: one 40-minute lesson: 3–4 ideas; 300–500 words; connected explanations and application; 5–8 activities.

## House rules
- **Assemble, never invent.** Notes, examples, activities, answers and classroom activities all come from `sources/`.
- Use the variety of sources: different notes, workbooks, drafts, teacher guides and past papers.
- Activity = exercise (the same thing).
- Examples (worked examples) only in Mathematics, English and Reading.
- One lesson = one 40-minute period. Lessons of kind `practice` are **Part B** lessons that continue the previous subtopic with new sourced activities (and examples where allowed).
- Maths in LaTeX; column sums and long division as coded figures.

## Files
- `sources/INDEX.md`: every source, its role and passage count
- `sources/<id>.md`: the passages (cite them by passage id)
- `scheme.json` / `scheme.md`: the accepted outline (once written)
- `lessons/`: lessons already written, so later weeks build on them
