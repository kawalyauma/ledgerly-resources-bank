# TOPIC: DISCIPLESHIP AND ITS REWARDS
## SUB-TOPIC: JESUS' TEACHING ON DISCIPLESHIP

### Meaning of discipleship
- A disciple is a follower of Jesus Christ.
- Discipleship means copying the examples of Jesus.
- Disciples imitate the work of Jesus as a teacher.
- Jesus calls disciples to serve other people.
- Christians should follow Jesus' teaching in daily life.

### Examples of discipleship
- Disciples should live in spiritual uprightness.
- Disciples should practise humility in daily life.
- Disciples should work for peace in their communities.
- Disciples should show courage during persecution.
- Obedience to God is part of Christian discipleship.
- Forgiving those who offend us shows discipleship.

### Requirements of discipleship
- Christians should be willing to suffer for Christ.
- Christians may give up selfish personal interests.
- Disciples should desire to serve other people.
- Christians should seek God's guidance.
- Christians should lead a life pleasing to God.
- Christians should become good examples to others.

### Christians who practised discipleship
- St Francis gave his property to the poor.
- St Francis lived a simple Christian life.
- Khama preached against murder and witchcraft.
- Khama encouraged his community to follow Christian teachings.
- Nicky Cruz left his former gang after becoming Christian.
- Mbaga Tuzinde is remembered among the Uganda martyrs.
