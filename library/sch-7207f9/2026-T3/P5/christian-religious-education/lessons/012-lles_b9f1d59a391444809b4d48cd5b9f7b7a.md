# TOPIC: OUR RELATIONSHIP WITH GOD
## SUB-TOPIC: WEEK 4 REVIEW

### Summary: God the Father
- The Holy Trinity means three persons in one God.
- God the Father created living things.
- God created non-living things.
- God provides people's needs.
- God protects people from danger.
- God sent his Son Jesus to save people.
- God the Father forgives sins.
- Christians should love God wholeheartedly.
- God's commandments guide Christian behaviour.
- Christians must not misuse God's name.

### Summary: God the Son
- God the Son saved people from sin.
- God the Son shows people God the Father.
- Jesus gives believers eternal life.
- Jesus healed people who were sick.
- Jesus fed hungry people.
- Jesus preached God's message.
- Jesus suffered on behalf of humanity.
- Jesus died on the cross for sins.
- The Son sent the Holy Spirit.
- The Spirit guides Christians in their lives.
