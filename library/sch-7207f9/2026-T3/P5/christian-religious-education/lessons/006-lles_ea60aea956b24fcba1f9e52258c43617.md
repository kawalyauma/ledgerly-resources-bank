# TOPIC: DISCIPLESHIP AND ITS REWARDS
## SUB-TOPIC: Week 2 review
### Summary: The meaning of discipleship
- A disciple is a close follower of Jesus Christ.
- A disciple follows the teachings of Jesus.
- Discipleship means copying Jesus’ ways of life.
- Discipleship means living according to Jesus’ example.
- Discipleship includes suffering for Christ’s sake and giving up our own interests. (Luke 14:25–27)
- Jesus taught about discipleship. (Matthew 5:1–10)
- Spiritually poor people are happy; the kingdom of heaven belongs to them.
- Those who mourn are happy; God comforts them.
- Humble people are happy; God gives them what he promised.
- Merciful people are happy; God will be merciful to them.
- Those who desire to do what God requires are happy; God will satisfy them fully.
- Pure-hearted people are happy; they will see God.
- Those who work for peace are happy; God will call them his children.
- Those persecuted for doing what God requires are happy; the kingdom of heaven belongs to them.
### Summary: Examples of discipleship
- Spiritual uprightness
- Being humble
- Being pure in heart
- Working for peace
- Courage during persecution
- Obedience
- Forgiveness
