# TOPIC: OUR RELATIONSHIP WITH GOD
## SUB-TOPIC: GOD THE FATHER

### The Holy Trinity
- The word Trinity comes from the Latin word Trinitas.
- Trinity refers to three persons in one God.
- God the Father is one person of the Trinity.
- God the Son is one person of the Trinity.
- God the Holy Spirit is one person of the Trinity.

### God the Father as creator
- God the Father created all living things.
- He created all non-living things.
- God provides people with their daily needs.
- God protects people from different dangers.
- God sent his Son Jesus to save humanity.

### How God the Father shows love
- God the Father provides the needs of his children.
- The Father forgives people who have sinned.
- God sent his only Son to the earth.
- God protects his children from danger.
- God loves people and cares about their wellbeing.

### How Christians relate to God
- Christians are taught to love God wholeheartedly.
- The first great commandment teaches love for God.
- Christians must avoid worshipping false gods.
- Christians should not misuse God's holy name.
- Christians should respect the day set aside for God.
- God's commandments guide the relationship between people and God.
