# TOPIC: DISCIPLESHIP AND ITS REWARDS
## SUB-TOPIC: The meaning of discipleship
### New words
Disciple, discipleship, happiness, persecution
### Meaning of disciple and discipleship
- A **disciple** is a close follower of Jesus Christ.
- A disciple follows the teachings of Jesus.
- **Discipleship** means copying Jesus’ ways of life.
- Discipleship means living according to Jesus’ example.
- A disciple imitates the work of Jesus as a teacher.
- Discipleship includes suffering for Christ’s sake.
- Discipleship includes giving up our own interests. (Luke 14:25–27)
### Jesus’ teaching about discipleship
- Jesus taught about discipleship. (Matthew 5:1–10)
- Jesus taught his followers about happiness.
- Spiritually poor people are happy.
- The kingdom of heaven belongs to the spiritually poor.
- Those who mourn are happy.
- God comforts those who mourn.
- Humble people are happy.
- God gives humble people what he promised.
- Merciful people are happy.
- God will be merciful to them.
- Those who desire to do God’s will are happy.
- God will satisfy them fully.
- Pure-hearted people are happy.
- Pure-hearted people will see God.
- Those who work for peace are happy.
- God will call peacemakers his children.
- Those persecuted for doing God’s will are happy.
- The kingdom of heaven belongs to the persecuted.
### Note
- Discipleship means following Jesus and living by his example.
- Jesus taught that several groups of people are happy.
