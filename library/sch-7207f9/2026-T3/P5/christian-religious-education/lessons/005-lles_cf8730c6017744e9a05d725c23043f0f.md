# TOPIC: DISCIPLESHIP AND ITS REWARDS
## SUB-TOPIC: Examples of discipleship
### New words
Spiritual uprightness, humble, pure, persecution
### Examples of discipleship
- Spiritual uprightness is an example of discipleship.
- Being humble is an example of discipleship.
- Being pure in heart is an example of discipleship.
- Working for peace is an example of discipleship.
- Courage during persecution is an example of discipleship.
- Obedience is an example of discipleship.
- Forgiveness is an example of discipleship.
### Examples in Jesus’ teaching
- Jesus taught about discipleship. (Matthew 5:1–10)
- Spiritually poor people are happy; the kingdom of heaven belongs to them.
- Those who mourn are happy; God will comfort them.
- Humble people are happy; God gives them what he promised.
- Merciful people are happy; God will be merciful to them.
- Those who desire to do what God requires are happy; God will satisfy them fully.
- Pure-hearted people are happy; they will see God.
- Those who work for peace are happy; God will call them his children.
- Those persecuted for doing what God requires are happy; the kingdom of heaven belongs to them.
### Note
- Examples include spiritual uprightness, humility, purity, peace-making, courage, obedience and forgiveness.
### Exercise
1. State two examples of discipleship.
2. Outline any three of Jesus’ teachings on discipleship.
3. How will God reward the following: the spiritually poor, the mourners, those who are merciful and those who are persecuted?
