# TOPIC: OUR RELATIONSHIP WITH GOD
## SUB-TOPIC: GOD THE SON

### God the Son in the Holy Trinity
- God the Son is one of the three persons of the Trinity.
- God the Father sent the Son to save people.
- God the Son showed people God the Father.
- God the Son brings eternal life to believers.
- God the Son sent the Holy Spirit to believers.

### What God the Son does for Christians
- Jesus saved people from the power of sin.
- Jesus reveals the Father through his teachings.
- Jesus gives believers the promise of eternal life.
- Jesus sent the Holy Spirit to guide Christians.
- The work of Jesus strengthens Christian faith.

### How Jesus demonstrated God's love
- Jesus became a human being like other people.
- Jesus healed people suffering from sickness.
- Jesus fed people who were hungry.
- Jesus preached God's message to communities.
- Jesus suffered for the sake of humanity.
- Jesus died on the cross for people's sins.

### The Son and the Holy Spirit
- The Son sent the Holy Spirit to people.
- The Spirit teaches Christians about the Son.
- The Spirit helps people during their prayers.
- The Spirit guides Christians in their daily lives.
- The Spirit strengthens people's faith in God.
- The Holy Spirit helps Christians overcome sinful nature.
