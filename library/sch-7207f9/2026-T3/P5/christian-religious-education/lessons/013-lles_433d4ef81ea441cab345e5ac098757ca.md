# TOPIC: RELATIONSHIP WITH GOD
## SUB-TOPIC: The Holy Spirit and the believer
### The coming of the Holy Spirit
- The disciples received the Holy Spirit on Pentecost day.
- It took forty days for the disciples to receive the Holy Spirit.
- A strong wind came from the sky.
- The apostles spoke different languages.
- Flames of fire appeared on their heads.
### Symbols of the Holy Spirit
- Strong wind
- Fire
- Dove
### Duties of God the Holy Spirit
- Romans 5:5; 8:11–17 teach about God the Holy Spirit.
- He teaches us about the Son.
- He guides Christians in their lives.
- He helps Christians during prayer.
- He gives us Jesus to proclaim Jesus.
- He strengthens our faith in God.
### Importance of God the Holy Spirit
- He enables us to share the suffering and glory of Jesus.
- He gives us blessings.
- He makes us God’s children.
- He helps us overcome our sinful nature.
- He gives us courage to call God our Father.
### Note
- Pentecost day is when the disciples received the Holy Spirit.
- Strong wind, fire and dove are symbols of the Holy Spirit.
