# TOPIC: PEACE AND SECURITY
## SUB-TOPIC: Changing statements into passive voice — Past simple passive voice
### Remember
- was/were … by
- The policeman arrested the thief.
- The thief was arrested by the policeman.
- The security guard followed the madman.
