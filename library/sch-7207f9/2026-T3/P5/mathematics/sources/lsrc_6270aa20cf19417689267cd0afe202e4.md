Food security
This is the keeping of enough food for future use.
Importance of food security
 Humans can be fed more efficiently on plant-based diet as they require less water land
and crops than meat-based diet.
 They reduce the risk of conflict over the scarce water and grazing land.
 They prevent deficiency diseases that can spread in humans.
 They do not require human reliance on domestic animals for food when
survival of animals can be uncertain e.g in times of drought.
