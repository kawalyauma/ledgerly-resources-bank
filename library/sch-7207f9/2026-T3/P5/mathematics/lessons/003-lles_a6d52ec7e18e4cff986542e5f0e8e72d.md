# TOPIC: MONEY
## SUB-TOPIC: More about simple rates
### New words
- Cost, costs, equivalent
### Example 1
- The cost of 6 cups is sh. 1200. Find the cost of 10 similar cups
- $6$ cups cost sh. $1200$.
- $1$ cup costs sh. $\frac{1200}{6}$.
- $1200\div6=200$.
- $10$ cups cost sh. $200\times10$.
- $10$ cups cost sh. $2000$.
### Example 2
- 3 pencils cost sh. 1500. How many pencils will Abel buy with sh. 4500
- $3$ pencils cost sh. $1500$.
- $1$ pencil costs sh. $\frac{1500}{3}$.
- $1500\div3=500$.
- Sh. $4500$ buys $4500\div500$ pencils.
- Abel buys $9$ pencils.
### Note
- $\frac{1200}{6}\times10=2000$.
- $4500\div500=9$ pencils.
