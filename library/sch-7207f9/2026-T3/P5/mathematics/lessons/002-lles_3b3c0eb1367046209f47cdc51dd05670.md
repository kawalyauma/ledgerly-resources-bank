# TOPIC: MONEY
## SUB-TOPIC: Simple rates exercises
### Remember
- $1$ book costs sh. $500$.
- $3$ books cost sh. $500\times3=1500$.
- $5$ books cost sh. $2000$.
- $1$ book costs sh. $2000\div5=400$.
### Example 1
- Find the cost of 8 books if each costs 700/=.
- $1$ book costs sh. $700$.
- $8$ books cost sh. $700\times8$.
- $700\times8=5600$.
- $8$ books cost sh. $5600$.
### Example 2
- 3 cakes cost 2400/= what is the cost of one cake
- $3$ cakes cost sh. $2400$.
- $1$ cake costs sh. $2400\div3$.
- $2400\div3=800$.
- $1$ cake costs sh. $800$.
