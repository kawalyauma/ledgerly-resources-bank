# TOPIC: MONEY
## SUB-TOPIC: More about simple rates exercises
### Remember
- $6$ cups cost sh. $1200$.
- $1$ cup costs sh. $1200\div6=200$.
- $10$ cups cost sh. $200\times10=2000$.
- $3$ pencils cost sh. $1500$.
- $1$ pencil costs sh. $1500\div3=500$.
- Sh. $4500$ buys $4500\div500=9$ pencils.
### Example 1
- 5 books cost of 1,000/=. Find the cost of 12 similar books
- $5$ books cost sh. $1000$.
- $1$ book costs sh. $1000\div5$.
- $1$ book costs sh. $200$.
- $12$ books cost sh. $200\times12$.
- $12$ books cost sh. $2400$.
### Example 2
- 10 mangoes cost 5000/= what is the cost of 7 mangoes?
- $10$ mangoes cost sh. $5000$.
- $1$ mango costs sh. $5000\div10$.
- $1$ mango costs sh. $500$.
- $7$ mangoes cost sh. $500\times7$.
- $7$ mangoes cost sh. $3500$.
