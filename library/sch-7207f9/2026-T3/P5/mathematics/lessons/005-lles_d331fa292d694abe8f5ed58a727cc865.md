# TOPIC: MONEY
## SUB-TOPIC: Shopping bills and change — Shopping list
### New words
- change, expense, each, per, amount
### Shopping bills
- A **shopping bill** helps you know money spent on an item.
- A shopping bill also shows the total cost.
- **Change** is the money taken back home after buying items.
### Example 1
- Okello bought sugar, rice and cooking oil.
- Sugar: $2$ kg at sh. $3,200$ per kg.
- Sugar cost: $2 \times 3,200 = 6,400$ shillings.
- Rice: $3$ kg at sh. $4,000$ per kg.
- Rice cost: $3 \times 4,000 = 12,000$ shillings.
- Cooking oil: $1$ litre at sh. $3,000$ per litre.
- Total expenditure: $6,400 + 12,000 + 3,000 = 21,400$ shillings.
### Example 2
- Kataike had sh. $20,000$.
- She bought items for sh. $11,400$.
- Money taken home: $20,000 - 11,400 = 8,600$ shillings.
- Kataike's change was sh. $8,600$.
