# TOPIC: MONEY
## SUB-TOPIC: Buying and selling exercises
### Remember
- A shopping bill shows money spent on an item.
- A shopping bill shows the total cost.
- Okello's sugar cost sh. $2\times3200=6400$.
- Okello's total cost was sh. $21400$.
- The money Kataike took back home is called change.
- Kataike's change was sh. $20000-11400=8600$.
### Example 1
- Kamwine went shopping with sh. $30000$.
- She bought $2$ tins of blue band at sh. $3000$ each.
- She bought $4$ kg of rice at sh. $2600$ per kg.
- She bought $3$ loaves of bread at sh. $3300$ each.
- What was her total expenditure?
- Blue band: sh. $3000\times2=6000$.
- Rice: sh. $2600\times4=10400$.
- Bread: sh. $3300\times3=9900$.
- Total expenditure: sh. $10400+9900+6000=26300$.
### Example 2
- A man had sh. $50000$.
- He bought $2$ kg of sugar at sh. $1200$ per kilo.
- He bought $3$ bars of soap at sh. $4000$ a bar.
- How much change did he have?
- Sugar: sh. $1200\times2=2400$.
- Soap: sh. $4000\times3=12000$.
- Total expenditure: sh. $12000+2400=14400$.
- Change: sh. $50000-14400=35600$.
