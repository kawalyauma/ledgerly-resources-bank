# TOPIC: MONEY
## SUB-TOPIC: Simple rates
### New words
- Currency, rates, cents, shillings
### Example 1
- A book costs shs. 500. What is the cost of 3 similar books?
- $1$ book costs sh. $500$.
- $3$ books cost sh. $500\times3$.
- $500\times3=1500$.
- $3$ books cost sh. $1500$.
### Example 2
- 5 books cost sh. 2000. Find the cost of one book
- $5$ books cost sh. $2000$.
- $1$ book costs sh. $\frac{2000}{5}$.
- $2000\div5=400$.
- $1$ book costs sh. $400$.
### Note
- $3\times500=1500$.
- $2000\div5=400$.
