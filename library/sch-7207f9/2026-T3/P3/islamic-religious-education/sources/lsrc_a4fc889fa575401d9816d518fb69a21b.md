Customs:
A custom is the acceptable way of behaving in a given society.
Examples of customs.
(a) greeting people
(b) paying bride price
(c) naming children
(d) last funeral rites
(e) crying for a dead person.
(f) welcoming visitors at home.
Importance of customs.
(i) Greeting people: It creates friendship and a sign of respect.
(ii) Greeting helps people to feel happy.
(a) Paying bride price:
This practice is a thank you to the parents for looking after the girl well.
(b) Naming children:
 It helps to identify people of the same clan so that they don’t marry one
another.
 It helps to create good relationship among people of the same clan.
(c) Crying for a dead person:
It helps one to express one’s sorrow to the dead person.
(d) Last funeral rites:
 It brings unity among the family and clan members.
 It enables the property of the dead person to be looked after well.
 It is an occasion when people are supposed to stop mourning for the dead
person.
 It helps the bereaved family to forget the sorrow created by the death.
