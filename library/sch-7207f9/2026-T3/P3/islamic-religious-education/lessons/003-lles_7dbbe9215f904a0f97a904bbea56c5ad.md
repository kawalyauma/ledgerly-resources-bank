# TOPIC: HISTORY OF ISLAM
## SUB-TOPIC: Week 1 Review — Khadijah and the Revelation
### Summary: Events immediately after the first revelation
- Qur’an 96:1-5 gives the first revelation.
- Surah Al-Iqrah 96:1 says, “In the name of your Lord who has created all that exists.”
- The first five verses tell about creation and learning.
- Allah taught writing by the pen.
- Allah taught man what he did not know.
### Summary: Khadijah’s reaction
- Khadijah received the message wholeheartedly.
- She accepted everything the Prophet said about the revelation.
- Khadijah accepted Islam.
