# TOPIC: HISTORY OF ISLAM
## SUB-TOPIC: Events which Occurred Immediately after the First Revelation
### New words
Events – happenings
### First revelation
- Qur’an 96:1-5 gives the first revelation.
- Surah Al-Iqrah 96:1 says, “In the name of your Lord who has created all that exists.”
- The first five verses of Iqrah tell about creation and learning.
- Allah taught writing by the pen.
- Allah taught man what he did not know.
### Events after the revelation
- The Prophet Muhammad received the first revelation.
- The story happened in the cave immediately after the revelation.
- The first five verses of Iqrah were shared.
- Learners practised reading the verses of Iqrah.
### Note
- The first revelation is in Qur’an 96:1-5.
- Qur’an 96:1 begins, “In the name of your Lord.”
- The verses mention creation, the pen and learning.
