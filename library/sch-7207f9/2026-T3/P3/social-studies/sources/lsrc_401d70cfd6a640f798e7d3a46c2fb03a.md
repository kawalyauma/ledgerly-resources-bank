Lesson two
Wind
Wind is moving air.
Importance of wind
 Wind takes away bad smell.
 Wind is used to turn windmills
 Wind is used in winnowing
 Wind pollinates flowers
 Wind dries wet clothes
 Wind cools our bodies
Dangers of strong wind
 Wind causes soil erosion
 Strong wind destroys our crops
 Strong wind destroys buildings
 Wind increases the spread of air borne diseases like
chicken pox, flu, mumps and measles.
 Wind raises dust
 Strong wind breaks tree branches
